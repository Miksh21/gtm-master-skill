## Detailed case studies

### Case Study 1 — AirOps ($3M pipeline + $500K closed in 6 months)

**Industry:** Content-heavy B2B SaaS (AI workflows for content production)
**Engagement:** 6-month ABM campaign run by Ivan Falco at ColdIQ
**Results:**
- $3,000,000 in qualified pipeline
- $500,000 closed-won revenue (16.7% pipeline-to-close conversion — strong for enterprise B2B)
- Consistent month-over-month improvement in conversion rates
- Remaining pipeline still in various sales cycle stages at 6mo mark (total revenue will grow)

**TAM funnel:**
- 15,000 companies (broad TAM)
- → 800 target accounts (95% reduction after aggressive filtering)
- Filter criteria: 50-500 employees, SaaS/tech vertical, content-marketing-heavy GTM, Series A-C, NA or Western Europe

**Buying committee mapped (4 personas):**
- VP Marketing / CMO (budget holder, content output focus)
- Head of Content / Content Director (day-to-day operator, workflow friction)
- Head of Growth / Demand Gen (content → pipeline metrics)
- VP Engineering / CTO (technical evaluator, integration approval)

**Tool stack:** Clay (orchestration + Claygent for personalization) + Instantly (email) + Expandi (LinkedIn) + Fibbler (ad engagement tracking) + Attio (CRM) + Usermaven (analytics)

**7-step process:**
1. Start with best customers (analyze 3 dimensions: ACV, retention/expansion, sales cycle)
2. Build lookalike target list (15K → 800)
3. Map the buying committee per account
4. Personalize every touchpoint per persona × per account
5. Sync all channels into one system (ad engagement → outbound aggression; outbound replies → ad messaging)
6. Adjust messaging by buying stage (cold / warm / hot)
7. Iterate relentlessly (weekly review, replace under-performing ad creatives in 7 days)

**Timeline pattern:** Month 1 = baseline establishment. Month 3 = sharp messaging + pruned targeting. Month 6 = $3M pipeline.

**Source blog:** "How We Built an ABM Campaign That Generated $3M in Pipeline for AirOps" — also see `clusters/campaign-ops/knowledge/abm-operating-system-2026.md` for the framework.

### Case Study 2 — Aircall (3,655 new accounts in 6 months)

**Industry:** AI-powered communication platform serving 22,000+ companies (voice/WhatsApp/CRM-integrated)
**Engagement:** 6-month data + TAM unification project
**Results:**
- **3,655+ new accounts created**
- **20+ fields enriched** per record
- 1 unified, self-refreshing TAM (replacing 3 disconnected regional ICPs)
- 6 months full rollout + adoption

**The problem:** "Like trying to run a Formula 1 race with three steering wheels." Aircall was juggling ZoomInfo + Apollo + Salesforce + HubSpot + Lusha + Cognism with no unified TAM. Different regions had different ICP definitions, enrichment standards varied, sales ops manually fixed data issues before routing.

**The 3-phase approach:**
1. **Foundation — define before you automate.** Created a "field dictionary" — single source of truth for what every data field meant across systems. Industry tags + region codes + ICP tiers + intent signals — all defined, standardized, version-controlled. Built first version of TAM in **sandbox, not production**.
2. **Structure — automate the right way.** Clay as orchestration platform. Workflows automatically pulled + refreshed firmographic + technographic data from ZoomInfo + Apollo + Cognism. 20+ fields per account, automatically updating Salesforce, respecting governance rules.
3. **Activation — measure + iterate.** Self-refreshing TAM giving RevOps "full control and clarity, all without manual work."

**Key lesson:** The "sandbox → QA → production" approach. Saved weeks of rework later.

## Operational benchmarks (specific numbers worth memorizing)

| Metric | Number | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Email volume per mailbox | 50-100/day | Higher = spam-trigger risk; lower = under-utilization |
| LinkedIn DM / connection requests | 100/week | LinkedIn rate limit (per profile) |
| Mailbox warmup duration | 14 days minimum | Below this = poor deliverability + bounce risk |
| Cold message word count | 70-80 words | Above this = read rate drops |
| LinkedIn hook character count | Under 55 chars (under 140 max) | "See more" cutoff truncates longer hooks |
| Visual vs copy weight in LinkedIn post | 80% visual / 20% opening line | The decision to read happens here |
| Cold-to-warm reply rate multiplier | 3-5× from customer alumni | Concrete signal lift to expect |
| Outbound waste in $50K+/mo Google Ads | $3K-$8K/mo recoverable | What Search Terms Analyzer typically finds |
| Quarterly pipeline lift from customer alumni play | +15-30% | Without additional headcount/budget |
| AirOps pipeline-to-close conversion | 16.7% | Strong for enterprise B2B |
| ABM target list size | 500-1,000 accounts | Sweet spot for full personalization with 2-3 people |
| Reply latency for Tier 1 signals | Under 1 hour ideally | Signal decay starts after this |
| Job-change detection window | Days 14-45 from start | New role peak buying window |
| Customer alumni window | 90-180 days post job change | Onboarding mode, fresh budget |
