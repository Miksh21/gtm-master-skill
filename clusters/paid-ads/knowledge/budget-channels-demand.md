# Budget Allocation, Channel Selection, Demand Lifecycle

## The 5-Stage Demand Engine

Replaces TOFU/MOFU/BOFU because the traditional funnel stops at conversion; B2B needs the whole lifecycle. Grounded in Eugene Schwartz's awareness stages: Unaware -> Problem Aware -> Solution Aware -> Product Aware -> Offer Aware -> Most Aware.

| Stage | Outcome | Awareness | Offers | Tactics | KPIs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Create | Brand affinity + trust | Unaware, Problem | Education, thought leadership | Boosted content, paid social | Cost per consumption, blended cost/opp |
| Capture | Convert in-market buyers | Solution, Product | Demos, free trials | High-intent keywords, listings, retargeting | Pipe-to-spend, direct cost/opp, ROI |
| Accelerate (sales-led) / Activate (PLG) | Close faster / convert free users | Product, Offer | Testimonials, case studies, events | Sponsored social | Pipeline velocity, paid signups |
| Revive | Restart closed-lost | Offer | Incentivized demos, guided trials | LinkedIn conversation ads | SQOs created, blended cost/SQO |
| Expand | Customer revenue | Most Aware | Referral and certification programs | Sponsored newsletters, partnerships | Influenced SQOs, expansion revenue |

**Build bottom-up for fastest ROI:** Expand -> Revive -> Accelerate -> Capture -> Create. You do not need the full funnel at once.

| Stage | Budget needed (audience size) | ROI timeline | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create | High | > 90 days | High (needs content strategy + POV) |
| Capture | Moderate | < 45 days | High (stiff competition, expensive) |
| Accelerate/Activate | Low | Varies with cycle | Low |
| Revive | Low | < 45 days | Low |
| Expand | Low | < 60 days | Medium (small audiences cap scale) |

Illustrative $30K/month allocations: product-led startup, large TAM = $15K Create (Problem Aware) + $10K Capture (Solution Aware) + $5K Capture (Product Aware). Sales-led, small TAM = $20K Create + $5K Capture (Product Aware) + $5K Accelerate (ABM on top accounts). PLG spreads across Create + Capture; sales-led small-TAM leans Create (thought leadership) + Accelerate (help sales close).

## Channel Selection

Five categories: paid social (LinkedIn, Meta, YouTube, Twitter/X, TikTok, Reddit, Quora), paid search (Google, Microsoft), paid review (Capterra, SoftwareAdvice, SelectHub), programmatic (display, digital audio, CTV, native), sponsorships (influencers, newsletters, events, podcasts).

Four selection criteria: targeting options (can you reach the ICP), media cost (CPC/CPM), reach (audience size with those options), policy (is your industry allowed). Pro tip: run a $100 test campaign on any channel you are seriously considering to learn real CPC/CPM before committing.

Channels by stage: Create = YouTube, LinkedIn, Meta, display. Capture = Google, Microsoft, Capterra, SoftwareAdvice. Accelerate/Revive/Expand = LinkedIn, Meta, display retargeting.

**Cross-channel budget split:**

| Total monthly | Split |
|---|---|
| $5-10K | 100% one channel; do not spread thin |
| $10-25K | 60-70% primary + 30-40% secondary (e.g. 70 LinkedIn / 30 Meta remarketing) |
| $25-50K | 3 channels (e.g. 40 LinkedIn / 35 Google / 25 Meta) |
| $50K+ | Full mix + experimentation budget |

## The Scaling Quadrant

X = effort (horizontal scaling, across campaigns), Y = budget (vertical scaling, raising budgets). AU audiences (high budget, low effort: more segments, size, frequency); GEO (high budget, high effort: new regions); AD (low budget, low effort: better creative and messaging, new ad types); O&B (low budget, high effort: objectives and bid strategies). No budget: work AD + O&B until efficiency frees budget. Budget but no time: work AU + GEO on existing assets.

**Securing budget from stakeholders:** pull the numbers (best channel by opps and cost, blended QoQ trend, pipe-to-spend and ROI), find the story (highlights, lowlights, journey, blockers, opportunities), present both together; data alone does not persuade and stories alone invite suspicion. Stakeholders analyze upside/downside like investors; build certainty.

## Campaign Planning

Four elements before launch: 1) names + objectives (naming convention determines future reporting; structure `[Region]_[Stage]_[Awareness]_[Objective]_[Persona]`, e.g. `NA_Capture_SolutionAware_Prospecting_ProductManagers`); 2) channels + budget; 3) locations + targeting (reference the ad persona doc); 4) creative + copy. Use bulk import/power editors for 10+ campaigns. Plans mean nothing until launched; keep them simple and repeatable.

## Ad Personas (ICP translated to targetable attributes)

An ad persona bridges "who we want" and "what the platform can actually target". Build one per persona by opening a draft campaign in each channel's ads manager and mapping what is targetable. Components: demographic (age, location, degrees), firmographic (industries, size, titles, functions, seniority, years of experience, skills), intent (dream keywords via Keyword Planner; review-site presence), consumption (groups, conferences, blogs, podcasts), psychographic (goals, challenges, what they are thinking/hearing/seeing, what they already tried). The psychographic block is what powers copy that resonates; the firmographic block is what powers the audience build.
