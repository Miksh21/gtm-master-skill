# Creative System (Meta-centered, applies broadly)

Creative is the #1 variable for Meta B2B performance. Post-Andromeda, creative is both the ad AND the targeting signal; targeting inputs are suggestions, creative is the real filter. Your hook is your filter. Your iteration velocity is your scaling ceiling.

## Buyer Situation Mapping (before any design work)

List 15-25 concrete buyer situations the ICP faces; each becomes a creative angle. Most companies test 3-4 generic concepts; winners test 15+ specific situations and find the 2-3 that hit the top 5% of in-market buyers. Situation = role + trigger + specific pain, e.g. "Finance director spending 3 days every month compiling cash position reports", "Founder who just raised Series A and needs to professionalize sales", "Controller at a PE-backed company asked for daily cash reports".

Sources, priority order: organic content winners from the last 12 months (market-validated for free), customer interviews ("what triggered your search", "what almost stopped you"), sales call recordings (objections, aha moments, exact pain language), support tickets, Meta Ad Library (competitor ads running 3+ months = likely profitable; steal angles and formats, never creative), community discussions.

## Hook-First Iteration Hierarchy

When iterating a winner, change elements in this order (descending impact):

1. **Hook** (first 3s of video / first line): the single highest-leverage variable; changing it changes who the ad reaches and can extend a winner's life 2-4 weeks. Hook rate target 20-25% (watched past 3s / impressions); below 15% the ad will never perform regardless of body.
2. **Visual treatment**: human faces lift engagement; 4:5 ratio generally beats square and landscape on CTR and CPA.
3. **Format** (static/video/carousel/UGC): reads as a "new ad" to the algorithm.
4. **Body copy / CTA**: lowest impact once someone is hooked.

Hook types that work: provocative question ("Why are 73% of CFOs still using spreadsheets for cash flow?"), specific pain ("Your finance team spends 15 hours/week on manual reconciliation"), counterintuitive claim ("The best ERPs are making your cash flow worse"), social proof lead ("[Company] cut close time from 14 days to 3"), number-first.

## Concept Types (test concepts, not micro-variations)

| Concept | Best for | Iteration potential |
|---|---|---|
| Problem/Solution | Cold, problem-aware | High (many pains) |
| Before/After | Visual outcomes | Medium |
| UGC / founder video | Trust, cold | High (different people) |
| Meme/humor | Pattern interrupt | Low |
| Data/statistics | Authority | Medium |
| Testimonial | Warm | High (different customers) |
| Case study | Proof | High |
| Comparison | Solution-aware, competitive | Medium |

Angles by stage: prospecting = problem/solution hooks, industry stats, founder-to-camera, contrarian takes. Remarketing = case studies with numbers ("43% churn reduction"), testimonials, demo walkthroughs, logos/G2, direct CTA ("You visited our pricing page, ready for a demo?"). Acceleration = competitive comparisons, ROI calculators, same-industry success stories, exec thought leadership.

Maintain 4-6 unique concepts per campaign at all times; with 1-2 the algorithm cannot learn.

## Three Pillars (every ad must pass)

1. **Messaging:** sell the click, not the product. Urgency via specificity: "How to fix your LinkedIn Ads ROAS in the next 60 days" beats "How to get started with LinkedIn Ads".
2. **Design supports the message:** visual reinforces copy; build per-placement sizes (Feed 1:1/4:5, Stories 9:16); 2-second legibility while scrolling; pattern interrupts beat polish.
3. **Analysis of WHY:** high CTR + low quality = wrong audience attracted. Low CTR + high quality = too niche, scale the audience. Track by concept, not just by ad.

## Format Playbook and Lifespans

| Format | Lifespan | Key rules |
|---|---|---|
| Static image | 14-28 days (fatigues fastest) | 4:5 1080x1350 Feed, 9:16 Stories; text under ~20% of area; refresh hook text on same visual buys 7-14 days; visually distinct or Meta flags duplicates |
| Video | 21-35 days | First 3s decide; captions always (sound off); <30s prospecting, <=60s retargeting; swap opening hook to extend; production styles (founder, testimonial, screen recording, animation) signal different audiences |
| Carousel | 21-35 days | 3-5 cards (10 max), first card must stand alone; reordering cards = "new" ad; add 1-2 fresh cards as cheap refresh |
| UGC / testimonial | 28-42 days (slowest fatigue) | Needs credibility markers (title, company, specific result); record 3-5 customers at once, cut 10+ variations; when fatigued switch customer, not concept |

B2B refresh reality: smaller audiences build frequency faster; plan a 14-21 day refresh cycle.

## Image-First Concept Validation

Prove new concepts as static images before investing in video: hours not days to produce, more concepts per cycle, a concept that fails as an image fails as video too. Flow: image in Testing Campaign -> Stage 1 delivery check -> Stage 2 quality -> if proven, produce video/carousel/UGC versions of the SAME concept -> scale the best format. Skip image-first only for video-native concepts (UGC, testimonials, product demos) and format-change iterations.

## Testing Cadence

| Action | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Delivery check (Stage 1) on day-7 ads | Wednesday |
| Launch 2-3 iterations on top performers (hook first) | Every 2 weeks |
| Launch 1-2 completely new concepts (from buyer situations) | Monthly minimum |
| Retire depleted ads (CTR -30% from peak OR frequency > 5.0; do not iterate) | When triggered |
| Iterate proven image concepts into new formats | On graduation |
| Fatigue signal check (frequency, CTR trend, CPM trend, relevance diagnostics) | Monday |
| Full refresh (re-audit buyer situations, new angles) | Quarterly |

What to build when an ad fails: delivery failure = new hook/visual/format (never copy). Zero pixel leads = abandon concept. Leads but zero QLs = keep format, change pain to filter ICP. QL rate < 40% = add revenue figures, industry terms. Cost > 1.5x TCPL = same angle, new execution. Fatigue = new hook + format + customer/color.

## Message Validation: Score Ads Against Revenue, Not Platform Metrics

The ad that makes money is often NOT the one with the best CTR/CPL. Process: launch 3-5 ads per buyer situation (ABO, equal budgets, 2-4 weeks). After each of the first ~20 sales calls, the closer scores the lead 0-3 on urgency, budget, fit (max 9), logged against the source ad. Sort ads by average quality score. Typical outcome: the meme ad with $18 CPL and 2.4% CTR scores 3.5 and loses; the ROI-calculator ad at $35 CPL scores 8.0 and wins. Scale the top 3 into ~10 variations each (keep angle, change format), move to CBO on the validated audience. Every 2 weeks: kill variations whose average quality drops below 5, add variations of winners, test 1-2 new angles monthly.

| Score | Urgency | Budget | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Just browsing | None, no authority | Not ICP |
| 1 | Pain, no timeline | Exists, unclear | Partial |
| 2 | Active problem, 3-6mo | Allocated for category | Good |
| 3 | Burning, now | Approved | Perfect |

## Fatigue Detection

| Signal | Threshold | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency cold > 4.0 / retargeting > 6.0 | Immediate action | URGENT |
| CTR -20%+ from baseline over 7 days | Creative dying | WARNING |
| CPM +30%+ over 2 weeks | Delivery struggling (or auction; check both) | WARNING |
| Ad Relevance "Below Average" any metric | Quality issue | WARNING |
| CPA up with stable targeting | Rule out other causes first | MONITOR |

Frequency bands by campaign type: cold safe 1.0-2.5, warning 2.5-3.5, critical > 4.0. Retargeting safe 2.0-4.0, warning 4.0-5.5, critical > 6.0. ABM safe 2.0-5.0, warning 5.0-7.0, critical > 8.0 (reinforcement is intentional, but diminishing past 5-6/person/week).

Weekly workflow: pull frequency (7d + 14d), CTR trend vs prior 2 weeks (-15-20% = fatigue; stable CTR + rising frequency = preemptive refresh within 7 days), CPM trend, relevance diagnostics; classify each ad healthy / warning (replacement within 7 days) / urgent (replace in 24-48h) / depleted (4+ weeks, frequency > 5.0, well below baseline: pause, never iterate).

**Rotation without resetting learning:** never swap creative inside a performing ad set; launch new ads alongside, or new ad set with same targeting; pausing does not reset learning.

**Library composition at any moment:** ~50% proven winners scaling, ~30% iterations of winners, ~20% new concepts. Pipeline stock: cold 4-6 active + 3-4 ready (refresh 2 weeks); retargeting 3-4 active + 2-3 ready (3 weeks); ABM 2-3 active + 2 ready (3-4 weeks).

Frequency control levers (Meta has no hard cap): audience size, budget pacing, more active ads, exclusions of recent converters (7-30d). Targets: prospecting 1-2 impressions/person/week, retargeting 2-4/week, ABM acceleration 4-6 per 2 weeks.

## Competitive Research and Organic Repurposing

Ad Library quarterly (top 3 monthly): record active ad volume, ads running 3+ months, dominant formats, angles, offers, CTA language. Learn angles/formats/offers; never copy creative or a big spender's scale strategy (a $1M/month account can run unprofitable market-capture campaigns; you need profit in 3-4 months).

Before creating from scratch, audit 12 months of organic: best LinkedIn posts, blog posts, webinar clips, testimonial quotes, influencer collabs. Organic winners repurposed as paid ads beat untested concepts because the market already validated the message.
