# Paid Ads Benchmarks (B2B SaaS, 2025-2026)

Reference ranges for sanity checks, not targets. Anchor to the account's own trend after 30 days.

## Meta B2B

| Metric | Benchmark | Strong | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR | 1.0-1.5% | 2.0%+ | < 0.8% |
| CPM | $10-20 | < $12 | > $25 |
| CPC (leads) | $1.50-2.50 | < $1.50 | > $3.50 |
| CPL (lead form) | $20-50 | < $25 | > $75 |
| Frequency cold | 1.5-3.0 | < 2.5 | > 3.5 |
| Frequency retargeting | 2.0-4.0 | < 3.0 | > 6.0 |
| MQL-to-SQL rate | 5-10% | 15%+ | < 5% |
| Landing page CVR | 8-12% | 15%+ | < 5% |
| Cost per qualified lead | $300-800 | < $400 | > $1,000 |

Seasonal CPM: Q1 lowest (scale aggressively), Q2 +10-20%, Q3 +15-25%, Q4 +60-80% (consider reducing B2B spend; e-commerce floods the auction). Reported cases when the system works: 100+ demos/month under $1,000 per demo, $2,000-4,000 cost per opportunity mid-market/enterprise. Advantage+ data (Meta's own): -33% cost per result vs manual, CBO -12% vs ABO, Advantage+ Leads -10% CPL.

## LinkedIn Overall

CPM $33-65; CPC standard $8-22+; TLA CPC as low as $4.14; Spotlight CPM ~$5.23 (74% below single image); conversation ads CTR 1-5%. Cost per engagement $2-5 (strong < $3); on-platform conversion $15-50; Lead Gen Form CPL $50-200; website form fill $200-500; SQL cost should stay under $500. Lead form fill rate target > 8%. B2B SaaS ROAS ~113% average and lagging 3-6 months; judge pipeline, not last-click.

**CTRTLP by funnel stage (CTR to landing page; for Engagement-objective campaigns LinkedIn's "CTR" column is engagement rate, not this):** cold 0.30-0.55% (platform average 0.45%), warm/retargeting 0.55-0.80%, engaged/BOF 0.80-1.30%. Above 1% is genuinely good; Message Ads target > 1.5%.

**Video ratios:** view rate (starts/impressions) > 40% horizontal; completion 8-15% horizontal, sub-3% vertical is normal; cost per 50% view $2-4 average, < $4 target. TLAs vs standard ads: 1.7x CTR, 1.6x engagement; vs company-page ads 3-6x CTR.

**Format cost-efficiency ranking:** 1 TLA, 2 video TLA, 3 video 10-30s, 4 document, 5 single image, 6 carousel, 7 Spotlight, 8 text (cheapest, always-on), 9 Lead Gen Forms, 10 conversation ads, 11 CTV (skip under $15K/month).

## LinkedIn Per-Format Medians (ZenABM 2026 report: 161,256 ads, $5.5M spend)

| Format | CTR | CPC | Eff. CTR to LP | Eff. CPC to LP | CPM | Dwell |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thought Leader Ads | 2.68% | $2.29 | 0.29% | $3.06 | $49.37 | 6.63s |
| Single Image | 0.42% | $13.23 | 0.42% | $13.23 | $59.15 | 3.64s |
| Lead Gen Form | 0.45% | $12.33 | n/a | $811.09/lead | $75.59 | 3.18s |
| Carousel | 0.32% | $13.30 | 0.31% | $13.30 | $45.28 | 4.56s |
| Document | 0.30% | $12.05 | n/a | n/a | $72.02 | 3.15s |
| Video | 0.24% | $15.61 | 0.24% | $15.61 | $38.94 | 3.91s |

Reading rules: grade link-driving formats (image, carousel, video) on **effective CPC to landing page** (cost / landing-page clicks), not raw CPC; raw CPC bills for likes and expands that never reach the site. TLAs get graded on both because they diverge ($2.29 vs $3.06). Lead Gen Forms are judged on cost per lead; documents on CTR/CPM/dwell. TLAs without a link legitimately have no LP metrics ("n/a (no link)", not a failure). TLAs at $3.06 per LP click are ~4x cheaper than image ads; under-investment in TLAs is usually the biggest single fix.

**Pause thresholds (after 1,000+ impressions, ~2/3 of format median CTR):** TLA below ~1.8%, image ~0.30%, carousel ~0.21%, document ~0.20%, video ~0.16%. An ad is decaying if weekly CTR fell three weeks running OR sits below its pause threshold past 1,000 impressions.

**Ad-count delivery floor:** LinkedIn needs >= $25/day per ad to optimize; per-ad daily spend < $25 = Too Thin, $25-49 = Watch, >= $50 = Healthy.

## ABM Program Benchmarks

Stage conversions: Identified -> Aware 55%, Aware -> Engaged 30%, Engaged -> Pipeline 20%, Pipeline -> Won 25%+; net ~2.5% of accounts to pipeline, ~0.6% closed. LinkedIn ABM channel: match rate 70-85%, CPM $30-80, CPC $8-15, account CTR 0.4-0.8%, cost per engaged account $150-300. Pipeline per $1 spent: $10+ healthy. Personalized 1:1 name/logo ads: 5-10% CTR vs 0.5-1% standard. ABM list targeting: ~50% cheaper CPM than native targeting. Retargeting: CTR 0.5-0.8% (2x prospecting), conversion 2-4% (3x prospecting), CPC 20-30% below prospecting.

## Audience Size Minimums

Meta: lookalike seed 500 accounts (1,000+ optimal), broad 500K-2M, ABM list 1,000+ accounts, ABM retargeting 100+. LinkedIn: 300 matched members hard floor, 1:1 ad set band 300-1,000 (max ~2,000), cold campaigns 15K minimum, cold sweet spot ~50K (10-20K on small budgets), MOF 1K-30K, BOF 1K-5K.
