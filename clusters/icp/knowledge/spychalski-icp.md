- (single-case: one B2B SaaS client) Use retention duration, not win/loss, as the ICP target variable: feed closed deals that ran their course into the LLM analysis and look for what predicts long tenure. In the worked case, inbound skewed 90% early-stage accounts that churned at 3-4 months while rare enterprise fills retained ~5 years; volume pointed at the wrong ICP. [spychalski/gtm-engineering-2026]
- Under ~100 closed-won deals (early stage, high ACV), use test messaging as the ICP proxy: run distinct messaging at candidate segments and read response rate as evidence the segment feels the pain. A fallback only, until retention data exists. [spychalski/gtm-engineering-2026]
