## Validate qualification empirically
- **ICP Prompt Loop:** stop hand-writing list-qualification prompts — use AI to write & refine them, validated by **3 consecutive clean rounds of 10 real companies** (human-verified ground truth; any miss resets the counter) before running the full list. Makes prompt quality provable, not assumed. [#88]

## Anchor on closed-won, not hypothesis
- **6-step closed-won seed system:** export 30-50 won deals → vector-similar companies → signal scoring → Claude tiering → waterfall enrich → tier-matched outreach. "The companies already paying you are the only ICP that matters" — scale lookalikes with signal-gated tiering that controls personalization depth + channel intensity. [#71]
  - Evidence (2026-10-11): build a dossier for every deal including lost, sort into won / lost / 10X (lifetime revenue >= 10x median payer), and ask how you could have known before inbound; avoids survivorship bias. [#1889]
- **Pick ICP data points from customer data:** run AI over existing customers to find the few points separating best from rest, spot-check manually, then enrich only those; a handful, not 12-20. [#1841]

## Segment over firmographics
- **"The list is the message":** segment by who your prospect sells TO (their customer ICP), not just firmographics — that's what changes the copy. Decision rule: *if a data point changes what you say, split it into a separate campaign.* [#86]
- **Segment-matched social proof on solution pages:** cross-link case studies to ICP-segmented solution pages so enterprise prospects see enterprise proof, SMB sees SMB proof. Reduces skepticism at the evaluation moment; compounds as the library grows. [#114]
  - Evidence (2026-10-11): (single-case: HeyReach) rebuilding site copy around 3 ICPs (agencies, sales teams, GTM engineers) with role-specific value props lifted free-trial-to-paid from 9% to 17%. [#1445]
  - Evidence (2026-10-11): match proof to the recipient's role, not just industry: a line that converts martech founders falls flat with PR contacts. For real-economy buyers match geography too (Texas oil and gas wants a Texas reference). [#607]

## Agents as buyers (emerging ICP/content shift)
- **Optimize for AI agents as buyers, not just humans.** Ramp's $44B raise signals agent-led purchasing going mainstream — if procurement flows through agents, messaging, pricing pages and structured data must be LLM-legible, reshaping ICP and content strategy. [#136]
- **Claude Code mentions in B2B job descriptions up 3x** (Kyle Poyar, 120K-JD analysis) — rapid enterprise adoption as a named tool in hiring specs; useful for ICP/persona targeting and positioning around AI tooling. [#157]

## Counter-narrative for objection handling
- **AI-native ≠ no sales motion.** Replit, one of the most AI-native GTM teams, still hires more salespeople and keeps legacy systems (Salesforce). Counters the "AI-native companies abandon traditional sales" narrative — useful framing for objection handling. [#146]

## Modern SDR hiring profile
- **Founding SDR rubric for upmarket = intelligence + team player + industry expertise, NOT dial volume.** "100-dials-a-day, at least upmarket, is completely dead." Three-criterion rubric replaces activity-metric thinking with judgment/learning criteria. [#163]

## Creator-radar 2026-10-11
- **Thiel channel dead zone:** around $1K deal size is too expensive for ads/virality yet too cheap for a salesperson; check the price band before choosing channels and escape it via price or motion. [#1237]
- **Workload materiality:** score "has the workload" separately from "the workload is core to their business" so big logos with an immaterial use case stop inflating pipeline. [#1300]
- **Disqualify fringe pains:** if you cannot put a number on the change, or the pain lands as a legal notice (accessibility, cookie consent), buyers solve it any way but buying; target pain core to the business. [#1959]
- **Rank a horizontal market by delta:** upside of fast response minus downside of not having it. 24/7 and legally compelled businesses (flood repair, regulated) beat nice-to-have buyers like dentists. [#1960]
- **Customer-research stopping rule:** keep interviewing a vertical until you can say the buyer's lines before they do (~300 conversations over six months in the source case). [#1797]
