## LinkedIn Sequence — l3mpire/claude-skills/linkedin-sequence  ·  filed 2026-07-31
Source: https://github.com/l3mpire/claude-skills · cluster: sequences
2-message post-connection DM sequence.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master LinkedIn word caps — sequences/tasks/write-linkedin-sequence.md:30-38]: gtm-master allows follow-up 1 up to 150 words and follow-up 2 up to 100. This skill caps EVERY DM at 40-70 words and states that anything longer reads as an email pasted into a chat window (the single biggest DM killer). Both sides — gtm-master: <150 / <100; LinkedIn Sequence: 40-70 hard, shorter always better.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master fixed-calendar cadence — sequences/tasks/design-multi-channel.md 14-day flow, "Day 3: LinkedIn — if accepted, send follow-up message 1"]: this skill triggers M1 on the acceptance EVENT (send immediately) and branches M2 on behaviour — 2A within 24h if they replied, 2B at day 3-5 if not, with 2B forbidden from referencing M1 at all. Both sides — gtm-master: day-offsets fixed across channels, one follow-up 2; LinkedIn Sequence: event-triggered M1 plus a reply/no-reply fork with two distinct M2 bodies.
- 🆕 new-angle: absolute content bans on M1 — no company name, no product name, no mention of a call or meeting, no "thanks for connecting", no "I wanted to reach out because", never more than one question, never open WITH a question. M1's only job is to earn a reply; M2's only job is to deepen or earn a soft next step. Neither may pitch.
- 🆕 new-angle: email-vs-DM doctrine table (context, length expectation, tone, pitch tolerance, follow-up expectation, subject line) used to justify the constraints — "they connected, they did not opt in to a pitch", so pitch tolerance on LinkedIn is strictly lower than in the inbox.
- 🆕 new-angle: per-seniority DM calibration — VP/C: a strategic observation that reframes something they think they already know; Manager: name a friction they live with daily; IC: a hyper-specific job moment. Same three tiers as gtm-master's ATL/BTL but with a distinct opening MOVE per tier rather than a tone shift.
- 🆕 new-angle: the no-reply bump is a clean-slate NEW angle that must not reference the earlier message — switch to the personal credibility/career lens, a market or timing trigger, or a resource useful regardless of the product. gtm-master's follow-up 2 adds a new angle but assumes continuity with message 1.
- ✅ supports [design-multi-channel.md + write-linkedin-sequence.md]: LinkedIn M1 must attack a different pain than Email 1; a LinkedIn reply pauses the email sequence; "LinkedIn leads on tone, email leads on depth" restates gtm-master's "email is for the argument, LinkedIn is for the relationship"; no emojis (they signal automation); connection note 300 chars and often better left empty; never fabricate a resource or case study that wasn't provided.

## LinkedIn Outbound Angle — l3mpire/claude-skills/linkedin-outbound-angle  ·  filed 2026-07-31
Source: https://github.com/l3mpire/claude-skills · cluster: sequences
Per-prospect profile analysis → 1 primary + 2 backup angles. Operating-mode caveat before reading: this is a MANUAL per-profile read across 10 dimensions with no volume or economics gate, while gtm-master handles this at list level (cold-email/tasks/personalize-at-scale.md). Promotion should carry an explicit "Tier-1 / ABM accounts only" boundary — it cannot run at list scale.
- 🆕 new-angle: tenure-band interpretation table as a timing signal — new role <6 months = honeymoon, wants quick wins; 6-18 months = building the business case; recently promoted = needs results fast; long tenure in the same role = either very happy or very stuck; new certification in a relevant tool = actively improving, receptive; liked a competitor's content = actively evaluating. gtm-master's signals cluster covers job change and hiring but not tenure bands, certifications, or competitor-content engagement.
- 🆕 new-angle: the headline treated as the highest-intent self-authored signal (role-based vs value-based headline reveals self-image and priorities), and recommendations read for WHO wrote them (peer / manager / direct report) as an influence map.
- 🆕 new-angle: angle taxonomy split into signal-based (recent post / new role / promotion / hiring / career pivot / certification / funding) vs persona-based fallback (identity, peer proof, cost of inaction, speed, credibility, curiosity), with a strict selection order — active signals always win; persona angles only when the profile is thin.
- 🆕 new-angle: mandatory "what NOT to say" and "topics to avoid" per prospect, plus a personalization-evidence appendix (exact profile quote → how it informed the angle) so the operator can verify the angle rather than trust it.
- 🆕 new-angle: a 0-10 ICP fit score (title/seniority 3, size/stage 2, industry 2, trigger 2, tech 1) with a 0-4 "outside your core ICP, proceed only if you confirm" stop. This is a FOURTH scoring scheme alongside validate-against-icp (/12), lead-scoring (100-point) and the Lead Research Assistant take's 1-10 — redundant rather than contradictory; reconcile to one rubric if promoted, don't add.
- 🆕 new-angle: thin-profile protocol — say so plainly, don't invent. Output a persona-based angle with a confidence caveat and recommend enrichment (Clay / company page / other channels) before sending.
- ✅ supports [write-linkedin-sequence.md + copywriting-principles.md]: never "I came across your profile", never open with a compliment or "hope this finds you well"; 300-char connection note; soft CTA, never "can we jump on a call?"; match their register (formal if they write formally); one idea per message; no ROI or percentage claims without a verified source; no client name-drops without permission.
