## LinkedIn campaign family (campaign-complete, limits-warmup, success-factors) — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/alex-vacca/linkedin-campaign-complete (+ linkedin-limits-warmup, linkedin-success-factors) · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: sequences
Family entry: 3 skills covering LinkedIn outreach volume, warmup, and campaign structure. gtm-master's sequences cluster (write-linkedin-sequence.md, design-multi-channel.md) has copy craft and channel roles but publishes NO numeric platform limits, warmup protocol, or account-health thresholds. Most of this family is net-new for the cluster.
- 🆕 new-angle: hard limits pack: 400 connection requests/month (non-premium), 800 open InMails/month (premium), ~120 total interactions/day ceiling; recommended daily: 10-15 connection requests, 15-20 DMs, 30-40 open InMails, 40 other interactions; keep pending invites under 500 (500-700 warning, 700+ danger). Interactions include profile views, likes, comments, endorsements, group joins.
- 🆕 new-angle: warmup protocol for new/dormant/restricted accounts: days 1-10 max 60 interactions/day all manual, days 11-20 up to 80/day with 5-10 light connection requests, day 21+ gradual ramp to full limits; no automation for the first 2-3 weeks; restriction recovery = stop all automation, 2 weeks manual-only, then slow reintroduction.
- 🆕 new-angle: human-pattern sequence inserts warm touches BEFORE the connection request: view profile (day 1) → like a recent post (day 1-2) → connection request (day 2-3) → first message 4-24h after acceptance → follow-up day 5-7. gtm-master design-multi-channel starts the LinkedIn track at the connection request (Day 0), no pre-request warm steps.
- 🆕 new-angle: activity hygiene rules: send only 9 AM - 6 PM in the PROSPECT's timezone, weekdays only (Mon-Thu best), at least one full day off per week, no sudden volume spikes, vary copy per target.
- 🆕 new-angle: with-note vs without-note connection tradeoff stated explicitly (note = higher acceptance, no-note = higher volume, slightly lower acceptance) plus an 8-audience template pack keyed to source (profile viewers, post engagers, event attendees, content commenters, newsletter subscribers, group members, warm intro requests, connection-acceptance openers). Canon has one generic connection-note recipe.
- 🆕 new-angle: target tiering by conversion likelihood: Tier 1 = webinar/event attendees, post engagers (ICP-filtered via Clay), company-page followers, profile viewers; Tier 2 = new group members, newsletter subscribers, commenters; with "Posted within last 30 days" as the mandatory active-user filter to avoid wasting the 400/month budget on dead accounts.
- ✅ supports [sequences/tasks/write-linkedin-sequence.md]: no pitch in the connection request, 3-4 sentence messages max, 2-3 follow-ups then stop, acceptance benchmark pivots at ~30% (swan: >30% healthy, 25/35/45 acceptable/good/great; canon: <30% after 100 sends = diagnose note or targeting), conversation-first not selling.

## Reach out — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/ido-goldberg/reach-out · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: sequences
Swan's master outreach verb plus its 9 motion reference pages (targeted, website-visitor, cold-list, linkedin-engagement, company-signals, inbound-hand-raiser, event-follow-up, intent-signals, improve-outreach). The parent carries strategy; each motion page carries do/don'ts and an org-rules accretion section. This is the densest sequence-design source in the Swan corpus.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master signal-as-opener, cold-email/tasks/write-first-email.md:24,39 ("Lead with the signal... signal reference, specific" in sentence 1) and cold-email/knowledge/copywriting-principles.md:69-71,147 ("The observation becomes your opener... 'Hey, I was looking at {{company}} on LinkedIn, and I noticed...'"), plus signals/knowledge/signal-scoring.md:118 ("Personalized email referencing pricing" for pricing-page visitors)]: the hook here "informs the angle; it rarely appears in the copy", tracking-type signals (website visits, intent data, profile views) "NEVER appear in the message" ("surveillance framing burns trust permanently, and multiple mature orgs ban it independently"), and public events get at most one clause of acknowledgment with the CONSEQUENCE led ("expansion into DACH usually means the compliance stack gets rebuilt within two quarters" beats "noticed the expansion into DACH"; "don't open with 'I noticed' or 'I saw that'"). Both sides. gtm-master: the observed signal is the opener, including tracked pricing visits; Swan/Ido: tracked signals set timing and topic backstage only, public signals appear as inferred consequence, and the sole exception is the inbound hand-raiser, where naming their action verbatim BUILDS trust because they know they did it. The identity-of-the-signal distinction (tracked vs public vs self-declared) is the synthesis canon lacks.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master congratulations play, signals/knowledge/timing/job-change-tracking.md:44 (best trigger template opens "Hey congrats on the new role at {{company}}") and signal-scoring.md:119,175 ("Congratulations + re-engagement", "Day 0: LinkedIn connection request + congratulations")]: the company-signals page bans it: "Don't congratulate... 'congrats on the funding' is now a recognized automation tell — mature orgs ban new-job and funding congratulations as openers outright"; the new VP "is drowning in congratulations; the director inheriting the agenda reads their inbox", target the person who OWNS the consequence, often not the person in the news. Both sides. gtm-master: congratulate then bridge; Swan/Ido: one clause of acknowledgment max, lead with the operational consequence (funding = a plan the money must deliver; exec hire = a 90-day agenda and no vendor loyalty; hiring spree = capacity landing before process; launch = a team newly accountable for adoption).
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master cross-channel referencing, sequences/tasks/design-multi-channel.md:23 ("After LinkedIn connection → reference it in the email sequence ('I saw we connected on LinkedIn')") and the default multichannel 14-day flow]: cadence here is "single channel unless the motion says otherwise, never referencing one channel from the other", default 3-5 steps at days ~0/3/7/14, and multi-thread only for ~200+ employee accounts, committee signals, or on request. Also: connection requests go out blank by default (vs write-linkedin-sequence.md's crafted 300-char note), and "don't chase an ignored connection request over email; being followed across channels is worse than being missed" (vs design-multi-channel's day-5 fallback to email-only). Both sides. gtm-master: coordinated dual-channel by default with explicit cross-references; Swan/Ido: one channel, invisible seams, escalation only by motion.
- 🆕 new-angle: the final gate, five checks run on EVERY message before staging, where a fail means rewrite not tweak: (1) delete the opener, does the message still make sense? ("the personalization is decoration; rebuild around the hook"); (2) could this go to anyone else this week? ("then it's a blast in costume"); (3) one CTA, 60-110 words, follow-ups shorter, subject 2-5 words lowercase internal-looking, more "you" than "I/we"; (4) is the proof a number or a name? ("'helps teams like yours' is not proof"); (5) read as the recipient with a full inbox: any reason to reply beyond politeness?
- 🆕 new-angle: examples-vs-rules precedence with a stated mechanism: approved sends accumulate automatically as style examples, but "examples carry the voice; this skill carries the strategy — when they contradict, the skill is right, because examples can't be unsaved and lag every course change." Course-correction flow: write the new direction into the skill FIRST (rules outrank examples), then refresh the pool with 1-2 newly-approved examples whose saved reasoning names the new strategy so retrieval surfaces them ahead of the old.
- 🆕 new-angle: feedback routing as a four-step procedure (the improve-outreach page): fix the draft first → generalize the reaction into a one-line do/don't keeping its condition ("still cringe" about a bonding line becomes `don't name the relationship, no "fellow founder"`; "keep it under 60 words for CFO types" becomes `to CFOs: under 60 words`) → file it in exactly one of three homes (one motion's page / the org-wide voice section / one sender's instructions; team-wide is the default, personal only on explicit "me/my") → confirm what was written and where. Silent edits count as feedback: diff the user's rewrite against the draft and propose saving the standing preferences. "Feedback that isn't written down is retraining that never happened."
- 🆕 new-angle: voice bootstrap ladder when no voice exists: CRM+email connected → extract patterns from the last 90 days of threads that got replies or booked meetings ("three winning threads sharing a pattern is a pattern; one is noise", record loser patterns too: "'tried quick question 40 times, zero replies' is as valuable as a winner") → email only: sent-folder replies → neither: user pastes 3-5 cold emails that booked meetings → nothing: derive a provisional voice from the website, flagged as soft. Per-sender instructions never homogenized ("a founder and an SDR don't sound the same").
- 🆕 new-angle: per-motion laws worth keeping verbatim: cold-list, personalization moves to the slice and persona ("a workable slice shares one real, checkable thing"; one skeleton per persona, ONE varying line per contact, "an honest segment email beats fake intimacy"; "slice drift is how a working play becomes spam"); intent-signals, third-party intent needs a corroborating second signal which becomes the visible hook while intent stays backstage, strong zone under 14 days rising, past 30 days context-not-trigger, "the researcher may be writing a blog post"; website-visitor, move same day, translate pages-viewed into the implied problem plus an independent checkable reason it's plausible, ask softer (a breakdown or comparison beats a meeting ask, "let them stay anonymous one step longer"), fold repeat visits into existing timing instead of a second touch; event-follow-up, depth tiers by engagement (met-in-person 1-2 highly personal touches; engaged 2-3; registered-only up to 3 honestly framed; non-ICP a connection request at most), sender = whoever actually attended ("an event follow-up from a name they never saw is cold email in costume"), never fake booth presence, never name the attendee list as the source, pre-event = blank request 5-7 days out + one short no-pitch note; inbound hand-raiser, respond in minutes-to-hours, quote their own words, stop any running cold sequence the moment they convert ("a cold cadence firing at a now-warm lead is the worst version of this motion"), answer first sell second, no "Re: [form name]" subjects, single real reply then let the human carry it; linkedin-engagement, 48-hour window, authors outrank reactors, first DM under 500 chars, engage the substance of what they wrote, product earns its mention later.
- 🆕 new-angle: sequence hygiene rules: every follow-up is shorter than the last, stands alone, and brings a genuinely new angle "or gets skipped"; last touch is a 25-50 word breakup with the door open and no new pitch, "and once sent, honored"; never the same email twice into one company; existing unsent sequences get edited, never duplicated; a channel missing its contact data gets fixed or dropped; drafts ship in the language the org sells in ("a wrong-language sequence is an error, not a style choice"); NEVER auto-send unless said in this conversation; never attach files.
- ✅ supports [cold-email/tasks/write-first-email.md hook discipline + write-followup.md new-value rule + coldiq-playbook.md soft CTAs]: hook ladder trigger > relationship > their content > company initiative > persona pain (usable but declared); no hook = push back before drafting, never invent a signal; one researched detail reacted to rather than exhibited; one attributed proof point sized to the company; altitude pitching (C-level hears risk/strategy, VP performance, director operational pain); offering something concrete beats asking for time.
