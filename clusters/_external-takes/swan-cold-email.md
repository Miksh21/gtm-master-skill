## Outreach messaging — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/maja-voje/outreach-messaging · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: cold-email
Value-first cold email built to earn replies: the reader gets something useful from the email itself whether or not they ever reply. R.E.P.L.Y. framework + 4-touch stack + intake questionnaire.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master open-rate benchmarking, cold-email/knowledge/coldiq-playbook.md:122 ("Open rate: 50%+" as a target metric)]: here "optimize and report on reply rate and positive-reply rate. Open rate is a vanity metric distorted by Apple/MPP." Both sides. gtm-master: open rate 50%+ sits in the benchmark table (though its A/B rules already say track reply-to-meeting, not opens); Swan/Voje: opens are not a reportable KPI at all, and the canon table should carry that asterisk.
- 🆕 new-angle: the value-delivery test as the core bar: "if they read your email and did nothing else, did they still walk away with something worth 20 seconds? If deleting your email costs them nothing, you led with a pitch, not value." Five concrete value forms ranked by what you can make most specific: an insight about their world / a specific observation about them / a real benchmark number ("most enablement teams we see take 6-8 weeks to get a rep productive") / an ungated resource / a mini-audit ("you already did a small piece of the work: spotted three gaps, drafted one example"). Two hard rules: the value must be IN the email, not promised on a call ("'I have some ideas to share' is a withheld pitch, not value"), and never fabricated ("a modest true observation beats an impressive invented one"), the value doubles as the credibility proof.
- 🆕 new-angle: R.E.P.L.Y. as the five moves per email: Relevance (a why-now signal; "if you genuinely can't find a why-now, you usually don't have a reason to send yet") · Empathy (their pain in THEIR words from voice-of-customer intake) · Payoff (the value delivered inline, the heart of the email) · Low-friction ask (one small reversible step "answerable in one line from a phone") · You-focused (run the you:we sentence count and rewrite if "we" wins). "A message that nails Relevance, Payoff, and the Low-friction ask will out-reply a beautifully written but generic one."
- 🆕 new-angle: intake gate before writing: minimum inputs are the persona, the problem in the buyer's own words, one piece of real proof, the signal, and one thing to give the reader for free; a thin brief gets questions before drafting, and a forced draft ships with its assumptions flagged for correction, "the most common failure is writing confident copy on top of guesses; the second most common is having nothing of value to say."
- 🆕 new-angle: the 4-touch value stack (days 1/4/8/13) where every touch delivers standalone value and "no touch should require having read the previous one": signal + insight → proof/case angle → different pain + ungated resource ("want me to send it?") → pattern-interrupt breakup with a parting useful thought ("wrong person?" / "should I close the loop?"). Cold-vs-warm register switch: warm/referred contacts drop the pitch posture entirely and can carry a direct ask, "a warm email that reads like a cold template wastes the warmth."
- 🆕 new-angle: framework-bends-to-context clause: length, formality, and ask flex by deal size, regulation, and relationship ("email that works for a $200/mo PLG tool differs from a $250k enterprise committee sale... when a client's reality contradicts a default here, follow the client's reality and note why"), the principles hold, the tactics bend; session flow = map the stack table BEFORE writing lines, show message then a 2-line rationale (signal, pain, value, ask), build a persona x signal matrix noting what stays constant (proof, offer) vs what flexes.
- ✅ supports [copywriting-principles.md give-first rules + write-first-email.md anatomy]: 50-90 word first touch scannable on a phone; subject 2-5 words referencing the signal or problem, never the product; line order relevance → empathy → payoff → ask; link-light plain-text first touches; one ask; kill hedges, "just", jargon, and em dashes; self-edit checklist ending "would I reply to this?"
Status: holding
