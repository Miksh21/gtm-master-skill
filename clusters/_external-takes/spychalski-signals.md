Shelf-life (stated by author): "only alpha for the next 4-8 months past September 2026" → re-verify by 2027-03.
- ✅ supports [signals/knowledge/go-to-market-alpha.md — the signal-originality test]: "there's no alpha in using the same signal as every other company... if every company uses 'raised from YC', the target gets 20-30 near-identical pitches." Independent restatement of the "can a competitor buy this exact signal off the shelf?" test, with a build-your-own-custom-signal mandate.
- ✅ supports [signals/tasks/customer-alumni-play.md (3-5x reply)]: champion tracking (notify when a past customer/champion changes roles) as one of the highest-converting signals. Spychalski's number is stronger than canon: **90%+ conversion** on this play across clients. Only guard: seed the list with neutral-or-positive-sentiment contacts, and it works best for table-stakes tools people re-buy out of habit.
- ✅ supports [signals/knowledge/tool-setup-guides.md — RB2B]: website-visitor deanonymization via RB2B as a tried-and-true signal from startup to enterprise, with the explicit false-positive caveat ("it'll surface a barista as a CRM buyer") → always run deanon output through a qualification engine before it reaches a rep.
- 🆕 new-angle: **job listings are a text-mining signal, not a boolean presence signal.** The value is not "has a RevOps req = reach out"; it is the aggregate TEXT across hundreds of a company's listings (new tech to ramp, new initiative, new region), which paints an internal picture no employee would volunteer. Tool named: **Centreion** (Raphael) — job-listings aggregator with **historical** listings (hard to source elsewhere) that makes org-level inferences. Build it yourself or use them.
- 🆕 new-angle: **product / PLG usage as a first-party signal.** For PLG products with high free-signup volume (real client example: 70k signups/week), nobody notices when e.g. 15 people from one company sign up. A system that detects company-clustered signups (or feature-pair usage that predicts a third-feature purchase) drives an enterprise-upsell motion ("15 of your team already use us, consolidate onto an enterprise plan"). Also applies to renewals via Intercom/Slack/Gmail customer-service trend mining.
- 🆕 new-angle: **firmographic score and signal score must be SEPARATE scores used for different jobs** (see also spychalski-lead-scoring.md). Firmographic = slow-changing fit (industry, geo, headcount) for bucketing; signal = fast-changing, action-triggering (new RevOps hire, complementary tech procured, 10-K disclosure). Only the **signal** score fires the rep notification; the firmographic score never should. This is the stated fix for signal-noise fatigue — reps glaze over a Slack channel that pings 10-50x/day, so precision + weighting + funnel-depth ranking of signals is the whole game (a website visit outranks a fresh raise because it is deeper in the funnel; a "hiring for a CRM" req outranks "raised a round → will need a CRM" even at the same funnel depth).
