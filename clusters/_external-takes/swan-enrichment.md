## Account intelligence analyst — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/katya-tarapovskaia/account-intelligence-analyst · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: enrichment
Deep single-account research → seller-ready briefing (buying committee, talk tracks, discovery questions, next actions). gtm-master's enrichment cluster is Clay-ops machinery; a structured account-briefing artifact has no canon counterpart. Explicitly scoped: single-account intelligence, NOT portfolio ICP work ("who should we sell to?" vs "how do we sell to THIS account?").
- 🆕 new-angle: the nine-section briefing contract, saved as a dated markdown file: 1. executive summary (company overview noting cross-source discrepancies, financial summary, for bootstrapped companies note what that implies: efficiency-led growth, founder-led decisions, and 3-5 numbered key initiatives each citing its signal source) · 2. strategic priorities with evidence and buying-behaviour implication ("'Grow revenue' is not a strategic priority. 'Scale Salesforce-led CX delivery into repeatable packaged offers' is.") · 3. tactical opportunities mapped to those priorities with entry point and feasibility (natural fit / stretch / discovery-needed) · 4. recommended approach per opportunity: offering, fit rating, a 2-sentence talk track referencing a specific signal, and proof ("metric proof only" flagged when no case study exists) · 5. buying-committee map in three tiers with per-person role assignment (economic buyer / champion / technical buyer / user buyer / blocker with the why) · 6. industry trends, current within 6 months, each with a talk track · 7. discovery questions that "should never be answerable with a Google search", each with context and what the answer tells you about deal viability · 8. next best actions naming the person, what to send, why now, timing, plus a drafted 6-8 sentence outreach email for the top action with a time-bound ask · 9. conclusion: strongest fit, biggest risk, fastest path to a proof point, 3 sentences each. Quick-brief mode = sections 1, 5, 7, 8 only, mode always announced.
- 🆕 new-angle: calibrated-confidence labeling as a writing rule: confirmed signal (press release, job posting, filing) stated as fact; inferred signal (content themes, hiring patterns) stated as inference; speculation labeled hypothesis; gaps stated plainly ("no public financial data found") instead of padded; and fit called honestly, "the user trusts you more when you're honest about poor-fit accounts than when you spin everything positive."
- 🆕 new-angle: uploaded third-party research (Humantic AI reports, Clay/Apollo exports) is treated as the richest input BUT cross-checked against live data: "third-party research reports sometimes conflate companies with similar names, misattribute news, or reference outdated signals. Always verify key claims — especially contracts, financial data, and personnel... Catching a data error before the seller acts on it is one of the highest-value things this skill can do." Reports get an analysis layer added, never a reformat.
- 🆕 new-angle: data-priority order: connected tools first without asking (company enrichment → contacts by committee titles → CRM for existing engagement, with CRM status always reported in the exec summary because "net-new vs already-in-pipeline changes the entire approach") → uploaded files (read fully before searching) → ask the user last, and only for target, positioning service, relationship context, and the meeting being prepped.

## Research — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/ido-goldberg/research · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: enrichment
Swan's research verb: route the ask to one of nine facet sub-pages, pick depth, run cheap-before-expensive, compose a cited answer. The facet routing and stop rules are the exportable methodology.
- 🆕 new-angle: nine-facet routing table with a primary-facet rule: Company (default) / Person / AccountTeam (committee) / GTMmotion / News (last 30 days) / Domain (traffic + SEO) / Financial / TechStack / CompetitorMentions, one sub-page per call unless multi-facet is explicitly requested, and even then a primary is picked. Depth is read from phrasing: brief 3-5 lines is the DEFAULT; the dossier runs only on explicit depth cues or stakes (board prep, founder intro, exec outreach), "over-researching when a sentence would do is the most common failure mode."
- 🆕 new-angle: stop rules per facet: person research stops after 2 empty web fetches ("they're a low-public-profile person and you've learned that"); a prior brief under 30 days old is the base and only stale parts refresh; company what-they-do = homepage H1 + subhead + first product section + top CTA composed into one sentence, two fetches max; "if business events covered the surface area, no more web fetches."
- 🆕 new-angle: confidence and provenance rules for research output: every claim cites its source inline (tool, URL, or CRM record); soft signals (inferred revenue, motion type, runway) carry an explicit "est"/"looks like" hedge, "headcount x industry-avg as a revenue figure stated like a fact" is the named failure; lede first ("the user sees in 30 seconds what they came for"); never a raw dump (40 LinkedIn posts, 200 keywords, full enrichment payloads get synthesized, not pasted); durable facts (motion type, financial state, detected stack, ICP fit) tagged back onto the company record "so the next play inherits it."
- 🆕 new-angle: motion fingerprinting heuristics from the GTMmotion facet: the pricing page is the motion fingerprint (freemium/trial up front = PLG; NO public pricing page is itself a signal: pure enterprise sales-led); job postings reveal the rep org (SDR/BDR + AE openings = outbound+inbound; channel/partner-manager roles = indirect motion); detected tools map to motion (PLG-signal tools, lifecycle marketing, product analytics); mixed motions stated as mixed, "never force-fit a motion the evidence doesn't support."
- 🆕 new-angle: tech-stack detection ladder with an evidence bar: existing records → CRM custom properties → job postings ("highest-quality public signal") → website script tags and embedded tools (form/chat/analytics signatures) → LinkedIn engineering posts → paid enrichment last; "MUST cite a source per tool detected... inference from 'they're enterprise' is not evidence"; scan only the 2-3 stack categories that matter to the play. CompetitorMentions facet: alias list first (parent names, rebrands, abbreviations), then LinkedIn mention sweep classified into switching signal / pain language / comparison shopping / customer reference / noise, then review sites focusing on ≤3-star reviews from the last 90 days ("lowest-star recent reviews are switch-ready prospects"), 20-item slices with one-line summaries.
- ✅ supports [enrichment/knowledge/credits-and-pricing.md + waterfall-enrichment.md conditional-run discipline]: check what the workspace and CRM already have before ANY paid call; free preview tools before enrichment; enrich only when cheap signals left a real gap.

## Account research brief — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/amir-baldiga/account-research-brief · filed 2026-09-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: enrichment
A one-page pre-meeting / ABM account brief for an AE. It overlaps the existing katya-tarapovskaia account-intelligence-analyst take (evidence vs inference labelling, stating gaps plainly) and the ido-goldberg research take (inline citations), so those points are not repeated here.
- ⚠️ contradicts [enrichment/clay-engineer/phases/02-list-building.md:85]: swan makes workforce composition by function and seniority a core brief input ("A company that is 60% engineering sells differently than one that is 60% sales") and reads recent joiners by function as live budget evidence. Both sides: gtm-master lists "Department headcount" under Never Use as "not accurate at scale"; swan: composition says more than total headcount. The scopes differ (list qualification at scale vs one account's brief), but the two disagree on whether the data point can be trusted.
- 🆕 new-angle: positioning pillars. Cluster the company's own description, tagline and specialities into 3-6 named themes (product areas, target buyer, stated differentiators). Each pillar cites the exact source phrase so the AE can repeat back the buyer's own words ("Buyers relax when you describe them the way they describe themselves").
- 🆕 new-angle: cost gate before pulling people data. Confirm both the target titles and the number of employee records with the requester first, because skipping this "is how a routine brief turns into a surprise bill".
- 🆕 new-angle: hiring as a direction-of-spend signal. Recent joiners grouped by function show where budget is moving ("added four engineers and no marketers last quarter"). A null result also goes in the brief as a finding, e.g. "No leadership changes in twelve months".
- 🆕 new-angle: two output shapes from one run. Structured data feeds the next automation step and a short human brief goes to the AE, because the two have different readers and lifespans. The benchmark it names for the mediocre output is "a reformatted About page".
- ✅ supports [enrichment/tasks/build-table-architecture.md:63]: resolve the company to a stable identifier (its own URL slug or domain) before researching, and never trust a display-name match. A near-match name search leads you to research the wrong entity, "which is worse than researching nothing".
