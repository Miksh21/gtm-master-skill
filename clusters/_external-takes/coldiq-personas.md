## Claude Persona — takechanman1228/claude-persona  ·  filed 2026-08-24
Source: https://github.com/takechanman1228/claude-persona (skills/persona) · cluster: personas
- ⚠️ contradicts [`personas/tasks/build-persona.md` Pass criteria — "power phrases include words from actual job postings or LinkedIn activity, **not invented**"; Process steps 1-3 ground the persona in 5-10 real profiles + real JDs]: this skill's entire evidence base is synthetic. It generates N fictional personas from a sampling plan, runs each as a respondent, and then reports model-generated quotes under a **Notable Verbatims** heading formatted exactly like real research (`"[quote]" — Name, Age, Occupation`). Both sides — gtm-master: persona vocabulary must be lifted from real artefacts or it fails the task; claude-persona: invented vocabulary is the deliverable, mitigated only by a footer caveat ("virtual panel of N synthetic personas — directional signal only", "not statistically representative; use for hypothesis generation", "AI-generated responses may exhibit positivity bias"). Adjudication needed on whether synthetic panels may feed persona/copy work at all, and if so under what labelling — the two positions are compatible ONLY if synthetic output is fenced to hypothesis generation and never reaches a Pass-criteria persona file.
- 🆕 new-angle: **persona panels as a research method** — gtm-master's personas cluster only ever produces one persona document per role, read by a human. This inverts it: personas become simulated *respondents* you can interrogate (`ask` = open-ended motivations/barriers/language, `concept-test` = forced choice across concepts, messages, packaging, price framing). A cheap pre-fieldwork pass for questions gtm-master currently answers by asserting (which message resonates, who to target first, whether a value prop lands).
- 🆕 new-angle: **agent separation as a bias control.** Each persona answers in its own `claude -p` subprocess (`--tools "" --no-session-persistence --json-schema`), never in shared context, explicitly to kill anchoring bias, consensus bias, and style contamination; `--safe-mode` adds context isolation so the operator's CLAUDE.md, plugins, and hooks cannot leak into and bias persona answers. gtm-master has no equivalent guidance anywhere on running multi-respondent simulations, and the failure mode it names ("all personas give identical responses" = shared-context mode) is the exact thing a naive one-prompt panel would produce.
- 🆕 new-angle: **deterministic sampling plan before generation, then programmatic panel QA.** Counts are allocated by the orchestrator, not the LLM (`base = floor(count/segments)`, remainder distributed, verified to sum); each persona slot is pre-specified (segment, age bucket, gender, occupation tier, geography, category stance, ethnicity hint); an exclusion list of already-used names/surnames/occupations is threaded across segment batches to suppress cross-segment duplicates; then `validate_panel.py` hard-fails on count mismatch and duplicate names and warns on occupation duplicates, age spread, gender balance, and Big-Five near-duplicate pairs (flagged at cosine ~0.985). gtm-master has no set-level QA on a persona *collection* — only per-persona specificity checks.
- 🆕 new-angle: **synthesis-over-summary reporting rule** — never write one paragraph per persona, never dump per-respondent reasoning; always cluster across respondents. Report shape: Direct Answer → Key Findings → Where They Agreed → **Where They Differed** → Verbatims → Top Signals (theme phrases with frequency counts) → Caveats. "Where they differed" as a mandatory section is the useful import: gtm-master persona output has no field for intra-persona disagreement or segment splits.
- 🆕 new-angle: explicit cost controls on a fan-out — cheap model for the panel (`sonnet` default), expensive model for the one synthesis call (`report_model: fable`), `fallback_model` on overload, and a hard `max_budget_usd_per_call` cap, with run metadata recording actual model IDs and `total_cost_usd`. A pattern worth stealing for any gtm-master task that fans out.
- ✅ supports [`personas/tasks/build-persona.md` Common failures — "generic" personas fail]: independently lands on the same bar from the simulation side — "a 5-persona panel with rich, differentiated responses is more valuable than a 15-persona panel with generic answers", and drives variation from Big Five traits + education level + communication style, with an explicit instruction that not all personas should be positive or articulate and some should break stereotype.
