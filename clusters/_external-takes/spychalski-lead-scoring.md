- ✅ supports [SKILL.md lead-scoring tiers + sequences/knowledge/tiered-routing-template.md]: never tier on a yes/no threshold; every account should be Tier 1/2/3 (or more) with bandwidth allocated by tier — Tier 1 = SDR + human-in-loop manual effort, Tier 2 = automated sequence + calls, Tier 3 = fully automated, don't overthink.
- 🆕 new-angle: **three scoring-model archetypes, pick by compute budget and stakes.** (1) Deterministic — a math equation (funding 50-100M = X pts). Cheap; use when the ICP is millions of companies and agentic scoring is too expensive. (2) Agentic — an agent outputs the score from internal heuristics + a few named exceptions; "surprisingly good" and handles exceptions hard to encode. (3) In-between (The Kiln's usual default) — an agent RUNS the deterministic equation, applies the hard-to-encode exceptions, AND emits reasoning a rep can read. No single correct choice; it is a compute-vs-precision trade.
- 🆕 new-angle: **calibrate against known 10s and known 0s, not vibes.** Collect ~10-20 accounts you know are perfect fits and ~10-20 you know are terrible. The system must score the 10s at 10/10 and the 0s at 0/10 with zero disparity; where an agent misses, read its reasoning and close the gap to perfection. Only THEN feed the gray middle (accounts you'd only guess at) — if the agent consistently matches your guess or beats it on read-back, the model is trustworthy. This is a concrete calibration protocol beside canon's score→tier ladder.
- ⚠️ contradicts [SKILL.md "Lead scoring tiers (max 100)": 70-100 Tier 1 / 40-69 Tier 2 / <40 discard — a single fit score]: Spychalski insists **two independent scores** (slow firmographic fit + fast action-triggering signal) rather than one blended number, with only the signal score driving the SLA/notification. Both sides. gtm-master: one 0-100 fit score → tier → SLA. Spychalski: fit score buckets; signal score interrupts and can upgrade a Tier-2 account into the Tier-1 outreach queue when signals stack.
