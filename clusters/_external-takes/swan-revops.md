## Account health audit — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/jeremy-hurst/account-health-audit · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: revops
On-demand health check of a trial or customer account across five lanes: use-case fit, lead-scoring integrity, alerting coverage, messaging architecture, user adoption. Built for vendors whose product runs the customer's GTM workflows; the audit reads the CUSTOMER'S configuration inside your own platform.
- 🆕 new-angle: evidence-only language ban as a hard output rule: "never use 'likely,' 'probably,' or 'seems' — every statement must be grounded in data pulled in Step 1"; all data (usage, memory, call transcripts, CRM activity) gathered in parallel BEFORE writing a single section.
- 🆕 new-angle: the tier-persistence check method: (1) count accounts carrying a tier label, (2) count accounts whose status shows sequencing language ("sequence drafted", "queued", "awaiting approval", these definitively passed qualification), (3) compare, and report the gap as a hard number and percentage ("7 of 26 qualified accounts tagged — 19 missing, 73% gap"); then read the offending workflows' instructions to confirm whether they call the central scoring workflow before sequencing and name that as root cause. Partial persistence (some workflows tag, others don't) is called out as a real and common failure distinct from zero-tags.
- 🆕 new-angle: spend is NEVER flagged as waste from volume or percentage alone: the mandatory cross-reference pulls processing history for 30+ accounts, classifies each qualified vs disqualified, and flags waste ONLY when disqualified accounts show expensive enrichment firing AFTER disqualification, or no qualification gate exists at all. "High spend on enrichment is not waste if the ICP gate is working."
- 🆕 new-angle: dead-workflow detection with an exact threshold: anything with zero executions for 5+ days (configurable) "is not solving anything — flag it specifically" (named, with the day count); outreach workflows checked for connected senders and whether sequences are actually sending vs drafted-and-sitting; integration-dependent workflows checked for live executions.
- 🆕 new-angle: messaging architecture audited by READING workflow instructions word-for-word, never titles or usage counts: every outreach-drafting workflow listed by name with where its voice rules live; inline voice rules = architecture flag (correct pattern: play workflows own signal/hook/research, the central voice layer owns voice); an unconfigured voice-governance layer is an immediate flag; zero saved message examples for any connected sender is a HARD flag with exact counts per sender.
- 🆕 new-angle: output economics: per-section character caps (750 / 1,000 / 500 / 1,000 / 1,000 / 1,000), sections strictly scoped (alerting section never assesses sequence approval status, that belongs to messaging), and every flagged finding in the summary pairs with one concrete intervention mapped from a fixed failure→intervention table (dead workflows → re-enable session with the champion; binary scoring → tiering working session; persistence gap → wire workflows to call the scoring workflow; post-disqualification spend → repair the gate; non-actionable alerts → rebuild alert bodies with context + stakeholders + drafted outreach; inline voice → consolidate; zero examples → save approved sends; declining logins → champion check-in + onboard users).
Status: holding

## Expansion opportunity analysis — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/jeremy-hurst/expansion-opportunity-analysis · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: revops
Single-customer expansion deep dive: adoption read → commercial decomposition → two priced proposals → expansion play (pattern, committee, timing, motion). Runs on accounts already flagged as worth the effort, not as a portfolio pass.
- 🆕 new-angle: hard inactivity gate before any commercial math: zero usage signals in the last 30 days = STOP treating as expansion, flag churn risk, recommend a save motion, end after Part 1. "Never build an upsell play on a silent account."
- 🆕 new-angle: invoice decomposition as a critical rule: never report the monthly charge as one number, split platform/base fee from consumption fee and reconcile to the total; per-unit rate to 4 decimals; the included allocation must come from the PLAN DEFINITION because "a live balance field may have overage purchases merged in"; subscription pulled live from billing, "never from a cached mapping table — plans drift"; subscription start date cross-checked against CRM close date.
- 🆕 new-angle: proposal math anchored on the trailing 3 completed months (current partial month EXCLUDED): conservative option = trailing average x 0.90 at the current per-unit rate; growth option = trailing average x 1.50 at current rate minus a 10% commit discount; overage rate = 2.0x the option's per-unit rate; comparison table includes monthly effective spend, annualized, delta vs current run rate, and blended per-unit rate (Current column denominator = plan units + overage units combined); the trailing-average math is footnoted "so the customer can check it."
- 🆕 new-angle: commitment-averse framing swap: present the same math as a "bulk purchase" they "draw down" against, with a coverage estimate (pool ÷ stated monthly need), avoiding the words "annual", "contract", and "commitment", "the math is identical; the framing is not."
- 🆕 new-angle: five expansion signal patterns tested in order, each requiring cited account data: repeated overage purchases (3+ in a cycle at the standard overage rate); customer-initiated capacity request ("the warmest signal; treat as a commercial conversation, not support", and never pitch a specific plan in the first reply, get the trajectory call first); upgrades + aggressive overage (2+ upgrades AND multiple overages in 90 days); ceiling hit + roadmap signal (≥90% utilization AND a conversation pointing at higher future usage); strong relationship + low realization (senior believer + acknowledged under-utilization + point-tool usage, the play is use-case expansion, not volume). No forced matches: propose a NEW named pattern (title ≤40 chars, definition ≤300 chars) when none fit.
- 🆕 new-angle: committee and motion rails: each target's message maps to a role-relevant gap from the Part 1 whitespace map (practitioner = daily workflow gap; ops = data/process gap; GTM leader = pipeline gap; founder = point-tool-vs-platform framing), gaps must be audit-grounded never generic; relationship-led plays confirm the champion list with the expansion owner BEFORE building; timing calls are explicit (go now on customer requests same week and pre-renewal windows; wait on unresolved escalations, departed champions, one-hot-month spikes, "one hot month is not a trend"); every motion ends with an internal escalation at ~10 days of silence (to the owner with a suggested next move, "never another message to the customer"); zero placeholder numbers in sendable copy; one active expansion play per account, tag cleared on reply or close.
Status: holding

## Reverse ETL Activation — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/austin-hay/reverse-etl-activation · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: revops
- 🆕 new-angle: warehouse→GTM-tools sync discipline as 7 fixed steps: identity resolution BEFORE any write (match-confidence floor 0.90; sub-floor rows quarantined to review, never guess-created; many-to-one collapsed by deterministic rule, never duplicated), CDC diff so only real deltas move (unchanged rows never sync, protects API budgets + audit logs), field-level type/format guards (schema-fail rows rejected with reason, never force-written), suppression + consent gates applied AFTER matching (so every hold is logged with who and why), batch to destination limits (default 200/batch, backoff on 429s), mandatory dry run with a max-delete guard (intended removals >10% of audience = ABORT, never mass-delete on a bad upstream run), live sync + reconciliation report (expected vs written per object) with persisted sync state.
- 🆕 new-angle: sequencer enrollment is NEVER automatic off a warehouse sync; a bad upstream filter becomes a mass mis-send, so enrollment is always a separate explicitly gated step (default cadence: CRM near-realtime, ad audiences hourly, sequences on-enroll only).
- 🆕 new-angle: authority split per field: warehouse is authoritative for computed traits (scores, tiers, segments), CRM is authoritative for rep-owned fields; a sync must never overwrite a rep-owned field. If identity resolution cannot clear the floor for a meaningful share, fix the upstream model (missing keys), do not lower the floor.
