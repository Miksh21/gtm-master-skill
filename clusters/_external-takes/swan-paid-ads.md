## Google Ads audit — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/aditya-sriram/google-ads-audit · filed 2026-09-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: paid-ads
Adapted from GoMarble's paid-media skills. It is a read-and-recommend rulebook for Search, Shopping, PMax and keyword research, and it fills the gap left by gtm-master's audit task, which is LinkedIn/Meta only and has no Google audit procedure.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/reference/google-ads-notes.md:12 and :27]: swan treats informational queries as a hard reject. It puts "how to" in tier Q5 (negate right away, whatever the cost), and its keyword-research step bans the patterns "how to / what is / guide / tutorial" outright ("Informational keywords = auto-reject"). Both sides. gtm-master: problem-aware terms ("how to scale outbound") are rung 4 of the intent ladder, opened once rungs 1-2 convert, and research terms are negated "unless intentionally problem-aware". swan: informational intent is never biddable.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/reference/google-ads-notes.md:27 "waste (spend, no conversions, 3+ clicks -> negative)"]: swan's zero-conversion kill line is based on cost, not clicks. A term with 0 conversions and cost under 0.5x target CPA is Q2 "promising", one at 0.5-1x is Q3 "unproven" (keep running), and it only becomes Q4 "losing" at cost of 1x target CPA or more. Both sides. gtm-master: 3 non-converting clicks is enough to negate. swan: let it spend up to a full target CPA. At B2B CPCs of $8-40 against a CPL of $80-400+, these two rules cut very different terms.
- 🆕 new-angle: rank pressure vs budget pressure, as explicit gates. Rank is HIGH if any of: IS lost (rank) 30%+; Search IS 50% or less with rank loss above budget loss; Top-of-page 60% or less on Q1; Abs Top 20% or less on Q1. Budget is HIGH only if all of: IS lost (budget) 20%+; budget loss above rank loss; CPA at or under target; Q1-Q3 at least 60% of spend. The decision matrix maps these to moves: raise budget 10-20%, raise bids 5-10% or fix relevance, fix rank first, or hold. Any Q5 term means negate now.
- 🆕 new-angle: CPC inflation triage. Rising CPC plus rising IS lost (rank) = competition, and a bid increase is allowed only if CPA is at or under target. Rising CPC plus new Q4/Q5 terms = match-type leakage, fix with negatives. Rising CPC plus falling CTR = relevance, fix the ads, not the bids. False-scale test: do not raise budget unless CPC WoW% growth is below CVR WoW% growth. Only chase Top/Abs Top if CVR at top is at least 1.15x the rest; otherwise it is an "ego tax".
- 🆕 new-angle: "valid controls only" guardrail. Every recommendation must map to a real UI control: campaign budget, bid target, or pause/enable. Never "shift budget between keywords, devices or asset groups". Never change bids and budget in the same move. Budget steps are capped at 20% for Search/Shopping and 50% for mature PMax. Segment-level recommendations need at least 50 clicks and at least 5 conversions. Never scale on data under 7 days old.
- 🆕 new-angle: PMax gates. Maturity: 30+ days (ideally 60) or 50+ conversions before any optimization. Exception: pause early if ROAS is under 0.3x target after spend of 3x AOV. PMax/Search ROAS ratio: under 0.5x = poor, 0.5-0.9x = optimize before scaling, 0.9x or more = eligible to scale. Asset labels: LOW = replace with variations of BEST; PENDING = wait 14+ days; never remove BEST or GOOD. Cap at 2-3x the initial budget until 90 days profitable. New audience signals run in Observation mode for 14+ days before switching to Targeting. Smart Bidding floor is 50 conversions/30 days for PMax/Shopping vs 30 for Search.
- 🆕 new-angle: competitor campaigns are OFF by default. Turn one on only if CPA is at or under 1.2x target AND rank pressure is LOW. Data-inventory traps: PMax asset labels and PMax search-term insights are NOT in the default CSV exports (the Asset Report and Search Terms Insights have to be pulled from the PMax campaign page), and the "divide cost_micros by 1,000,000" rule applies only to raw API data, never to CSV.
- ✅ supports [paid-ads/reference/google-ads-notes.md:23,42]: fix the weak Quality Score component (expected CTR, ad relevance, landing page) rather than raising bids; 50 or more searches a month with explicit buyer intent is the floor for a keyword; Broad match only after conversion signal is proven.

## Meta Ads audit — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/aditya-sriram/meta-ads-audit · filed 2026-09-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: paid-ads
Adapted from GoMarble's paid-media skills. Six sequential skills (health check, choose the primary metric, detect campaign structure, ad set analysis, Pareto ad analysis, creative/video analysis). It is written for ecommerce first, with lead gen handled as a mode.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/knowledge/meta-b2b-system.md:33 "build a 1% lookalike... then expand to 2%" and meta-operating-system.md:87 "expand lookalike 1% -> 2-3%"]: swan hard-rules "Never suggest lookalike audiences in any context." Both sides. gtm-master: CRM-seeded lookalikes are the Tier 1 audience and a scaling lever. swan: lookalikes are banned outright, and the fix for an underspending ad set is to expand the audience.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/reference/meta-tracking-capi.md:42 "6-7 good (target)" and :75 "EMQ >= 6/10"]: swan sets the EMQ gate at 8.5. Anything under 8.5 is a CRITICAL alert: stop and fix the pixel before any optimization. Both sides. gtm-master: 6+ is the target and 4-5 means add parameters. swan: 6-8.4 blocks the audit.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/knowledge/meta-operating-system.md:49 "Spend < 3x TCPL: WAIT... 2x has a 13% false-negative rate" and :111 "0 conversions after 5-10x target CPA"]: swan's pause rule for an ad is "0 conversions AND spend > 2x ad set CPA target", and an ad set leaves learning once it has spent 2x AOV or 4x CPA, whichever is higher. Both sides. gtm-master: 3x is the minimum evidence, and kill only at 5-10x. swan: pause at 2x, the exact threshold gtm-master rejects as too noisy.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/knowledge/meta-operating-system.md:91 "Meta needs 50 conversion events per ad set per week"]: swan says the 50-conversion rule "only applies to brand-new accounts with no prior history"; for everything else the gate is spend-based (2x AOV or 4x CPA). Both sides. gtm-master: 50/week per ad set sets the minimum budget, (CPA x 50)/7. swan: accounts with history do not need it.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/knowledge/meta-operating-system.md:54 "Frequency > 3.5 is the canonical cold-prospecting fatigue trigger" and creative-system.md:91 "Frequency cold > 4.0 / Immediate action"]: swan says "High frequency alone ≠ fatigue". Fatigue requires frequency rising AND CTR falling over 14+ days. Swan also says frequency caps should never be used on conversion objectives. Both sides. gtm-master: a frequency threshold on its own triggers a swap. swan: it needs a CTR decline to confirm.
- ⚠️ contradicts [paid-ads/knowledge/creative-system.md:17 "Hook rate target 20-25%... below 15% the ad will never perform"]: swan's hook-rate bands are 40%+ good, 26-39% average, under 25% poor ("rebuild hook completely"). Caveat: the denominators differ. swan uses 3-second views / video plays; gtm-master uses 3-second views / impressions. Both sides. gtm-master: 20-25% is the goal. swan: 20-25% is the "poor" band. Before importing either number, pin down which denominator it uses.
- 🆕 new-angle: top-performer protection. Never recommend pausing the ad with the most conversions in an ad set, whatever its budget share; Meta pushing 60-80% of spend to it is optimization working. Pause matrix: 0 conversions and spend > 2x CPA target = pause; CPA > 3x the ad set average with 3+ conversions = pause; 1-2 conversions = watch; ad under 7 days old = watch. Only ad sets holding 25%+ of campaign spend are compared. "Scalable" requires 10%+ of account revenue and results not dominated by a warm/customer pocket of 80%+.
- 🆕 new-angle: Pareto triage. Pull all active ads, paginating through every page, and keep the ones that make up 90% of cumulative spend. Baseline against the Pareto averages. CPM 30%+ above average plus PCM (primary conversion metric) 30%+ below = pause. CTR 30%+ below plus low PCM = check comment sentiment first, then test stronger-CTA variations. Good metrics with a declining trend = new creative, not a budget change.
- 🆕 new-angle: video diagnosis tree. Hold rate = ThruPlays / 3-second views, benchmarked against a 90-day account average (good = average + 25%). Hook under 25%: rebuild the hook. Hook good, hold below average: keep the first 3 seconds and rebuild everything after. Hook and hold both good but CTR under 0.65%: fix the CTA. Variation order for winners: copy, then angle, then format, then hook.
- 🆕 new-angle: data-integrity traps. (1) OUTCOME_SALES optimized on COMPLETE_REGISTRATION is almost always a third-party purchase tracker (Profitmetrics, Triple Whale, Northbeam), not a misconfiguration. Ask once, and never recommend switching to PURCHASE, pausing, or cutting budget by more than 20% until confirmed. (2) Purchases from the API: use omni_purchase, falling back to purchase; never sum the overlapping action types (it inflates ROAS). CSV figures are already deduplicated. (3) Read the conversion event from promoted_object, never from campaign or ad set names. (4) API budgets are in cents.
- 🆕 new-angle: structure detection. Primary vs Testing is confirmed when the testing campaign has under 30% budget share and 5+ ads, and the primary has 40%+ share, fewer ads, and 70%+ of its ads also in testing. Bid Limit Split: identical targeting but different bids. Audience Split: different audiences. Anything else counts as a custom pattern, to be diagnosed through the creatives, landing URLs and attribution windows.
- ✅ supports [paid-ads/knowledge/meta-operating-system.md:26,87]: CBO putting 80% of spend into one ad set is the algorithm working and should not be treated as a fault; budget increases are capped at 20% per move; changing bid strategy or making non-trivial targeting edits resets learning.
