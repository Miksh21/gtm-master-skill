## SEO intel — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/alon-goldenberg/seo-intel · filed 2026-09-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: content-marketing
A six-play live-data SEO kit: keyword research, rank check, site audit, content gap, competitor reverse-engineering and AI visibility. It overlaps `content-marketing/knowledge/seo-topic-pipeline.md` and also `aeo-reddit/knowledge/aeo-measurement-system.md`.
- ⚠️ contradicts [content-marketing/knowledge/seo-topic-pipeline.md:11 and tasks/run-seo-topic-research.md:62]: seo-intel starts keyword research from the user's topic. It expands 15-25 seeds straight from that topic and sweeps the SERP, with no account-intelligence or transcript phase. Its content-gap play gets topics from competitor coverage: a topic where a competitor ranks top-10 is scored "CONFIRMED, high-value gap". Both sides — gtm-master: work that skips account context "produces a plan any competitor could have", and every opportunity must trace to a signal from calls or comms (`seo-topic-pipeline.md:31`); swan: the SERP and competitor pages are the source of opportunities, and business relevance is just a 1-3 multiplier in the score.
- ⚠️ contradicts [content-marketing/tasks/run-seo-topic-research.md:18 "Ahrefs (or equivalent) is required for volumes/KD/SERP data"; seo-topic-pipeline.md:119 feasibility tiers keyed on KD ≤40 / 41-65 / 66+]: seo-intel runs without an SEO tool. Difficulty comes only from what the SERP shows: Low means niche domains and under 1,500 words on average; High means authority domains dominate and pages run over 3,000 words; Very High means brands own the top 10 or the content type doesn't match. It also says "never quote search volume, domain authority, or traffic numbers you did not measure". Its score is (relevance x intent value) / difficulty, with Transactional=4, Commercial=3, Informational=2, Navigational=1. Both sides — gtm-master: tool KD and volume are required inputs, and a ≥50/mo volume filter applies; swan: tool metrics are optional, and SERP evidence alone is enough to score difficulty.
- ⚠️ contradicts [reference/aeo-and-ai-buyer-discovery.md:11 (skill root): `/llms.txt` + `.md` endpoints described as "direct AEO/GEO infrastructure"]: seo-intel rates a missing llms.txt as Low severity, "low priority, forward-looking". What it checks FIRST is robots.txt access for GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot/anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot and GoogleOther ("if bots can't crawl, nothing else matters"). Both sides — gtm-master: llms.txt is core supply-side infrastructure; swan: crawler access is the gate and llms.txt is optional.
- 🆕 new-angle: delta discipline for repeat runs. Keep a dated, append-only snapshot per domain. Last run over 14 days old means full baseline; a snapshot within 14 days means delta mode; a same-day repeat means ask before re-running. Findings are keyed `page_url|rule_id` and sorted into New / Worsened / Unchanged / Resolved. The exec summary shows only NEW+WORSENED, and resolved items count as wins. Rank moves: |Δ|≥5 major, 2-4 notable, ≤1 stable, plus new entry and drop-out. First-run rows are "BASELINE", never "NEW". gtm-master only says "re-run Phase 4 before building" and "revisit quarterly".
- 🆕 new-angle: content gaps validated against the SERP in two passes. Pass 1 is a light sweep of every gap for competitor presence. Pass 2 runs only on confirmed gaps and captures AI Overview, snippet and PAA. Competitor rank sets strength: top 10 = 3, 11-20 = 2, not in top 20 = 1, unvalidated = 1. Effort comes from competitor word count (<1,500 = 1, 1,500-3,000 = 2, 3,000+ = 3), plus 1 if they have a hub page. "Thin" means your coverage is under 30% of the best competitor's words. If under 50% of gaps were validated, say so in the TL;DR.
- 🆕 new-angle: honest hub detection when reverse-engineering a competitor. Count only body anchors between the H1 and the first footer marker. Drop any anchor that appears on more than 80% of pages. A page is a hub when 3+ same-theme pages link to it by exact normalized URL; never substring-match. Filter boilerplate H2s (conclusion, FAQ, TL;DR, next steps) before clustering themes. Delta triggers: 3+ pages under a new theme, blog velocity ±30%, keyword-rich anchor ratio moving more than 10 points.
- 🆕 new-angle: extra AI-visibility metrics missing from aeo-measurement-system. Citation rate = domain citations / brand mentions; many mentions with few citations means the brand is discussed but not linked, which is fixable. Share of AI voice, with divide-by-zero handled as "space may not trigger AI answers yet". A false-positive guard for common-word brands: count a mention only if a domain term is within 5 words or the domain is cited. "No AI answer" is recorded as an early-positioning opportunity. Queries are phrased as natural questions, except on Google AI surfaces.
- 🆕 new-angle: GEO priors and block sizes. Princeton GEO boosts: cite sources +40%, statistics +37%, expert quotes +30%, keyword stuffing −10%. Apply the 3-4 most relevant methods per page. Self-contained block sizes: 134-167 words for AI citation, 40-55 words for snippet/PAA, under 30 for voice. The format is chosen from SERP features: AI Overview → GEO passages; snippet/PAA → answer blocks + FAQ schema. Pages in the organic top 5 supply about 80% of AI Overview citations.
- 🆕 new-angle: a technical site audit, which gtm-master lacks entirely. Seven categories: meta, headings, JSON-LD schema, internal links, content quality (4-gram shingle Jaccard over 0.9 = duplicate), technical, and observed CWV. Every finding is tagged [C] confirmed, [L] likely or [H] hypothesis; CWV is always [H]. A rule firing on more than 10% of pages goes up one severity level. Grades: A = 0 critical and ≤2 high; F = more than 5 critical.
- ✅ supports [content-marketing/knowledge/seo-topic-pipeline.md:75,91,134]: never verdict without reading the live SERP, let the SERP's rewarded format lead (a content-type mismatch raises difficulty), and never invent metrics.

## Media and PR outreach — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/gali-firon/media-pr-outreach · filed 2026-10-26
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: content-marketing
- 🆕 new-angle: gtm-master has no earned-media or journalist outreach at all; four pitch types with hard length caps: first contact under 5 sentences with no ask (resource offer only), news pitch under 150 words, byline pitch built on an exec POV that already exists (a proposed specific headline plus "draft ready"), reactive pitch under 100 words sent within a few hours with a quote promised within the hour.
- 🆕 new-angle: anchor gate: every non-reactive pitch cites a specific, on-beat piece by that reporter verified recent at the source (title, date, link logged); no live anchor = reporter not ready; "love your coverage of X" is a beat, not an anchor; a reactive pitch anchors to the breaking story instead.
- 🆕 new-angle: them:us sentence ratio of 3:1 minimum (rewrite at 1:1), close on one concrete give (named exec quote on a stated angle, data point, exclusive, ready draft) plus one low-effort ask; never open or close with "happy to be a source"; the press release is supporting material, never the pitch.
- 🆕 new-angle: one reporter per outlet per story, no mass or templated sends, independent review gate then human send; exactly one follow-up after 5-7 business days, one sentence; log every response including declines. Optional warm-up before first contact: follow and genuinely engage with 1-2 posts, wait a few days (skip for reactive).
- ✅ supports [cold-email/knowledge/human-voice.md:34]: no "circling back" or sequence-narrating language, plain one-person-to-another voice.

## Visual argument review — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/gali-firon/visual-argument-review · filed 2026-10-26
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: content-marketing
- 🆕 new-angle: review B2B design assets (deck, one-pager, carousel, case study, proposal slide) on 8 dimensions in fixed order, structure before craft before polish: strategic fit, brand fidelity, story and hierarchy, layout, typography, proof and data design, imagery and assets, client-readiness. A failure high on the list usually explains failures lower down.
- 🆕 new-angle: output contract: verdict first (Client-ready / Close, needs fixes / Not ready, never soften Not ready into Close), what works (what to protect), ranked fixes each tagged High/Medium/Low with the exact slide or element, the specific change and the commercial reason; then brand and data checks; then one stronger idea that does not reinvent the asset. Match scrutiny to stakes (a LinkedIn graphic is not a proposal).
- 🆕 new-angle: blocking rules for client-facing assets: an unsourced or invented metric, a misleading chart (3D, truncated axis) or a generated or approximated real logo is High severity, not cosmetic; metric cards show number first, precise label second; charts carry units and source.
- 🆕 new-angle: craft heuristics: one message per visual unit with action titles ("Reach that holds through Q4", not "Results"), a 10-second scan reveals the argument, roughly a third of the canvas left empty, hierarchy from size, weight, spacing and placement rather than color (one accent color), dense means information-dense never small type. Toolkit: one vector icon set (never model-generated, never mixed sets), hero imagery generated from one anchor image then varied, no headline text baked into generated images.
