## Customer expansion scoring — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/ariel-cohen/customer-expansion-scoring · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: lead-scoring
Sub-skill scoring a paying customer for expansion (usage depth + persona quality + headroom → tier). gtm-master's lead-scoring cluster is pre-sale only; post-conversion scoring has no canon counterpart.
- 🆕 new-angle: the auto-created-artifact exclusion: anything onboarding auto-creates exists on virtually every account and counts for NOTHING as depth; depth is judged only by what the customer deliberately built (integrations connected beyond defaults, automations created beyond the default one, usage volume, multiple active team members). Crediting default objects as engagement is the named failure mode.
- 🆕 new-angle: hard inactivity cap: zero usage signals in 30 days caps the tier at second-lowest regardless of everything else, with churn risk explicitly flagged, "churn risk takes priority over expansion scoring."
- 🆕 new-angle: senior-stakeholder exception overriding seat-count logic: a VP/SVP/C-suite/Director-level registered user at a quality company ($10M+ funded OR 100+ employees OR known brand) scores second-highest tier regardless of team size or seats, because "this person can unlock budget and teammates."
- 🆕 new-angle: signal stack ranked for expansion specifically: completed meeting (new departments, budget conversations, advanced use cases) > product depth > chatbot/support advanced questions > post-signup website visits (pricing = plan-upgrade signal) > LinkedIn engagement with founder content (advocacy). Tier ladder: Diamond = deep multi-user usage + high ACV + "could 3-5x in ARR"; Gold = 1-2 integrations + medium-high ACV; Silver = shallow or low ceiling; Bronze = barely active, re-engage before expanding.
- ✅ supports [lead-scoring/tasks/score-and-prioritize.md persona-authority weighting]: persona quality as multiplier (VP building on the product beats a junior IC doing the same); output disciplined to tier + 2-3 actionable sentences citing all dimensions, never essay-length reasoning; parent ACV assessment referenced, never re-researched.

## Account progression staging — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/steve-armenti/account-progression-staging · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: lead-scoring
Deterministic account-stage assignment (Unaware → Aware → Engaged → Qualified → Sales Ready → Customer) from the full decayed signal stack, built to make attribution measurable as lift-vs-baseline. Direct overlap with lead-scoring/knowledge/awareness-stage-model.md.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master stage-advance criteria, lead-scoring/knowledge/awareness-stage-model.md:99 ("Each list uses OR criteria (not AND) — a single strong signal advances stage")]: here advancement past Aware is gated on a buying group: Engaged requires 2+ DISTINCT engaged contacts (default `{{MIN_CONTACTS}}`=2), Qualified requires 3 contacts across 2 departments (or a strong intent score), and "a single power user generating ten sessions is still one contact — that's Aware, not Engaged." Anonymous activity counts toward Aware volume only, never toward the distinct-contact gate. Both sides. gtm-master: single strong signal advances so action triggers early; Swan/Armenti: one loud champion can fake momentum, so account-level progression demands breadth (with one carve-out: a genuine hand-raise or booked meeting is Sales Ready regardless of breadth).
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master regression rule, awareness-stage-model.md:69 ("30+ days no activity → demote")]: here "never regress on a weak or new signal"; regression happens only when decay drops qualifying evidence below threshold AND the account passes the 90-day stale window, surfaced for review, or via explicit disqualification, "never as a side effect of routine scoring." Both sides. gtm-master: 30 days silence auto-demotes; Swan/Armenti: demotion is a reviewed event with a 90-day floor, not an automatic side effect.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master stage-count guidance, awareness-stage-model.md:167 ("5+ stages — overcomplicates the workflow; 5 is the right count")]: this ladder runs six stages (Unaware / Aware / Engaged / Qualified / Sales Ready / Customer). Both sides. gtm-master: five, and the model deliberately starts pre-opt-in; Swan/Armenti: six, adding an explicit Customer terminal state and treating labels as cosmetic while ordering and gates are the methodology.
- 🆕 new-angle: ICP fit is a hard gate on Qualified: "a non-ICP account never reaches Qualified on engagement alone", including on a strong third-party intent score. gtm-master's Considering stage has no ICP gate on entry criteria.
- 🆕 new-angle: evidence-or-it-didn't-happen: every stage change writes a stage-entry record (stage, entered_at, the exact rule that fired, a JSON evidence snapshot of the buying-group features, prior stage, time-in-prior-stage). "A stage with no evidence snapshot is a bug, not a stage." Determinism test: "if two operators can't reach the same stage from the same evidence snapshot, the rule is underspecified — tighten the threshold, don't add judgment."
- 🆕 new-angle: the measurement layer this substrate exists for: attribution as delta-versus-baseline, not touchpoint credit. Baseline = per-transition conversion rate + median/P75 time-in-stage; lift = exposed cohort vs matched unexposed control on ICP, starting stage, firmographics; `lift = (exposed_rate − control_rate) / control_rate`; wait ~90 days of stage history before publishing lift or "the numbers bounce and lose trust." Budget goes to what moves stage transitions, not clicks.
- 🆕 new-angle: tunable defaults exposed as parameters: ads >3 impressions/14d or ≥2 visits/14d for Aware; ≥2 key-page views or ≥2 email clicks/14d for Engaged; engagement half-life 30d, ads half-life 14d; stuck-in-Aware >30d feeds a batch activation list (not per-account pings); no stage change = no alert; only Sales Ready transitions page a rep, with buying-group summary + recommended next action.
- ✅ supports [awareness-stage-model.md stage-beats-score principle + priority-branch assignment]: exactly one stage tag, highest qualifying stage wins by precedence; write-verify-retry on the stage tag with honest FAILED status; identity resolution before staging (never stage an orphan event); Sales-Ready alert includes relationship history and unengaged exec stakeholders.

## Account tier scoring — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/amos-bar-joseph/account-tier-scoring · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: lead-scoring
Production Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond scoring: pre-conversion on intent + ACV potential, trials/customers on activation depth + headroom, with hard gates, floors, caps, and verified writes. The most complete scoring rails in the Swan corpus.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master pricing-visit weighting, signals/knowledge/signal-scoring.md:35 ("3+ pricing page visits in 7 days = 80 pts" → Hot, SDR sequence <24h, no persona requirement)]: here anonymous signals count as non-buyer persona at enterprise accounts (1,000+ employees): "even stacked anonymous pricing-page visits stay Silver"; Gold needs a named verified senior buyer persona or 2+ first-party signals from named people, and the Gold-tiebreaker never applies to anonymous stacks. Both sides. gtm-master: stacked pricing visits alone are a hot signal worth same-day outreach; Swan/Amos: at large orgs an unnamed visitor has no path to a buying conversation, so identity and persona gate the tier.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master decay mechanism, lead-scoring/tasks/score-and-prioritize.md:85 ("a list scored 60 days ago is no longer valid. Re-score.")]: here "never demote on a weak new signal", the full memory stack is scored every run, and demotion happens only through explicit gates (self-serve, disqualified market, vendor reclassification) or a deliberate periodic review, never as a scoring side effect. Both sides. gtm-master: scores expire on a calendar; Swan/Amos: tiers are sticky downward by design and only deliberate events lower them.
- 🆕 new-angle: the self-serve gate runs before EVERYTHING, no exceptions: take the LOWER of enrichment vs LinkedIn headcount ("enrichment providers return bucketed ranges whose midpoints inflate small-company headcount — exactly how a 27-person shop gets mis-scored as a Gold MQL"); verify against LinkedIn whenever the count is a range or within ~3x of the floor; funding alone never overrides the employee floor ("runway is not a buying motion"); a 6-person subsidiary of a unicorn is still self-serve (the entity's own buying authority decides); unknown headcount + no confirmed funding gates by default; resolved count within ~10 employees of the floor = never auto-launch outreach without explicit approval. A CEO hand-raise, stacked profile views, direct replies: "none change the employee count."
- 🆕 new-angle: prior-relationship Gold floor with a genuine-evaluation gate: Closed Lost or trial alumni get Silver-scoring signals raised to Gold, but ONLY when a prior deal, logged sales touchpoint, or trial record proves a real evaluation happened; a mis-tag, a vendor-selling-to-you record, or a disqualified-without-evaluating account scores on merits. Lifts Silver→Gold only, never Bronze→Gold, and never overrides the self-serve gate.
- 🆕 new-angle: signal-source verification before weighting: every named person's email domain checked against the company domain; mismatch = excluded from scoring; no email = half weight; >50% unverified names = hard Silver cap stated in the output. Plus the self-artifact guard: on signup/trial triggers, deals/tags/owners created by the current run never count as "rep already engaged", a deal counts only if its create-date predates the execution.
- 🆕 new-angle: named tier moves worth stealing: exhausting a trial's usage limit before the trial ends is a Diamond-level signal ("high-volume active usage under real constraint"); C-suite/founder registration at a high-ACV account scores Diamond not Gold ("the economic buyer arriving unilaterally"); a company hiring its FIRST ops/infrastructure role for the team you serve counts like 1-2 extra intent signals ("building before the stack locks in"); <3 dedicated buying-team members caps at Silver unless founder-registration or >$10M funding overrides; fresh-trial (<14 days) bypasses the inactivity cap; high-ACV trials floor at Gold but the 30-day inactivity cap beats the floor.
- 🆕 new-angle: borderline tiebreaker with a scope limit: genuinely borderline Silver/Gold defaults to Gold ("the owner would rather review a Silver-quality account than miss a real one"), EXCEPT when all signals are third-party: "an account with only post engagement is Silver, full stop." Post engagement is Silver-level alone at any volume; a lone profile view caps at Silver; profile view + second first-party signal qualifies for Gold only for buyer-persona viewers at medium+ ACV.
- 🆕 new-angle: write discipline: exactly one tier tag and one ACV tag per run, ACV committed and verified BEFORE tiering, tag writes re-read and retried once with `TIER_TAG_STATUS = VERIFIED | FAILED` carried into the alert ("never report a tag as applied that wasn't confirmed"); Bronze/Silver stay silent, Gold/Diamond alert in the org's one standard format; already-being-worked accounts route to the active-deals channel as one line instead.
- ✅ supports [score-and-prioritize.md persona + ICP + signal composite and signal-scoring.md multi-stakeholder weighting]: multiple buyer personas from one company = significant multiplier; ICP fit judged by motion and use case, not industry label ("classify Bronze only if you can articulate WHY your product can't help"); vendor/partner/investor exclusion checks with partner-signup exception; buying-committee enrichment to 3+ persona-matched contacts on alerting tiers.

## Score — swan-gtm/gtm-skills/skills/ido-goldberg/score · filed 2026-07-28
Source: https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills · cluster: lead-scoring
Swan's core qualification verb: a 2x2 (intent strength x deal size) producing Gold/Silver/Bronze, with routing sub-skills for new business, re-engagement, and expansion/PLG.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master re-scoring cadence, lead-scoring/tasks/score-and-prioritize.md:94 ("After 60 days → re-score (signal decay)")]: decay here is LAZY: "NEVER batch-rescore on a schedule — decay is lazy, triggered only by new signals. Dormant accounts age in place at zero cost." When a new signal arrives, Gold scored >60d ago with nothing since downgrades to Silver, Silver >90d to Bronze, and quiet Gold/Silver accounts get a staleness flag in the state summary instead of a rescore. Both sides. gtm-master: calendar-driven re-scores keep lists valid; Swan/Ido: scheduled rescoring burns credits on dormant accounts, so decay applies only at the moment a run happens anyway.
- ⚠️ contradicts [gtm-master multi-stakeholder scoring, signals/knowledge/signal-scoring.md:37 ("Multiple stakeholders from same account = 70 points")]: multi-contact signals here are interpreted, not summed: different functions engaging independently = org-wide momentum (qualitatively stronger than one person with the same volume); same function = team evaluation; the read is surfaced for the rep to challenge, "do not apply a formula — the goal is to make the pattern visible and reasoned, not to calculate a number." Both sides. gtm-master: fixed points for committee formation; Swan/Ido: the composition of the contacts changes the meaning, so it is a judgment surfaced with reasoning.
- 🆕 new-angle: the two-pass credit gate: Pass 1 qualifies from data already in hand only (Swan record, memory, CRM, the triggering signal, zero enrichment calls) into ICP-likely + rough tier; enrichment, contact research, and drafting run ONLY when ICP-likely AND preliminary Silver-or-above. "Bronze and Non-ICP accounts never trigger enrichment."
- 🆕 new-angle: `isInAnyTargetMarket` synced on the company record every run before any other write, keeping the platform's native qualification field aligned with the latest verdict; Non-ICP accounts get tagged Non-ICP (never Bronze), ICP accounts always carry a tier (Bronze floor, "never leave an ICP account untagged").
- 🆕 new-angle: signal hierarchy with an explicit stacking rule: direct inbound intent > completed meeting > product engagement > website visit (pricing/demo >> blog) > prior warm engagement (full weight within 90 days, positive reply at the strong end, event registration at the weak end) > conversational interaction (weighted by commercial specificity) > business event (third-party, "a moment of change, not confirmed intent") > social (lowest); "two lower-hierarchy signals can outweigh one higher-hierarchy signal"; third-party alone can reach Silver but Gold requires at least one first-party signal.
- 🆕 new-angle: routing modes as first-class: warm-context flag (prior positive engagement + new signal = re-entry, framing acknowledges the relationship); refresh mode for in-pipeline rescoring where rep-confirmed intel overrides public data and the return is a change delta ("Score unchanged — no new material intel" short-circuits all writes except memory); re-engagement mode requires a MEANINGFUL CHANGE (new leadership, competitor displacement, funding, a signal addressing the loss reason, >12 months elapsed, new inbound from a different person), a new signal alone returns Bronze "monitor only", and with no recorded loss context two change signals are required. Silver/Gold re-engagement returns a resurrection-first framing block: what changed, what to acknowledge from the prior deal, recommended first-touch angle.
- 🆕 new-angle: alert semantics per tier: Silver alerts exist for score VERIFICATION (full signal stack posted to a QA channel, "purpose is score verification, not action"); Gold alerts are the action alert; Bronze/Non-ICP log only. Tech stack is a multiplier never a substitute: "strong tech stack + no intent → note it, do not inflate the tier."
- ✅ supports [score-and-prioritize.md bias and audit trail]: when ambiguous, err toward the higher tier ("a rep reviewing a Silver-quality Gold is better than missing a real one" mirrors canon's economic-buyer override instinct); every run writes a structured CRM note + prepended memory snapshot (signals strongest-first, persona, deal size, change delta) as the human-readable audit trail.
