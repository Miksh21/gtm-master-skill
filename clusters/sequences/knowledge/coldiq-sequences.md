Boundary for every bullet below: manual per-profile reads, Tier-1 / ABM accounts only; they do not run at list scale. [l3mpire/linkedin-outbound-angle]
- Tenure band as timing signal: new role <6 months = wants quick wins; 6-18 months = building the business case; recently promoted = needs results fast; long tenure in same role = very happy or very stuck; new certification in a relevant tool = receptive; engaged with a competitor's content = evaluating. [l3mpire/linkedin-outbound-angle]
- Read the headline (role-based vs value-based) as the highest-intent self-authored signal of priorities, and recommendations by who wrote them (peer / manager / report) as an influence map. [l3mpire/linkedin-outbound-angle]
- Angle selection order: an active signal (post, new role, promotion, hiring, pivot, certification, funding) always wins; persona-based angles (identity, peer proof, cost of inaction, speed, curiosity) only when the profile is thin. Output 1 primary + 2 backup angles. [l3mpire/linkedin-outbound-angle]
- Every angle ships with an evidence appendix (exact profile quote -> how it shaped the angle) plus per-prospect topics to avoid, so the operator verifies the angle instead of trusting it. [l3mpire/linkedin-outbound-angle]
- Thin profile: say so, do not invent; send a persona angle with a confidence caveat and enrich (Clay, company page, other channels) before sending. [l3mpire/linkedin-outbound-angle]
