- LinkedIn limits: ~400 connection requests/month (non-premium), 800 open InMails/month (premium), ~120 total interactions/day; daily working range 10-15 requests, 15-20 DMs, 30-40 open InMails, 40 other interactions; pending invites under 500 (500-700 warning, 700+ danger). Interactions include views, likes, comments, endorsements. [alex-vacca/linkedin-campaign-complete]
- LinkedIn warmup for new, dormant or restricted accounts: days 1-10 max 60 interactions/day all manual; days 11-20 up to 80/day with 5-10 light requests; day 21+ gradual ramp; no automation for the first 2-3 weeks; after a restriction, stop automation, 2 weeks manual only, then reintroduce slowly. [alex-vacca/linkedin-campaign-complete]
- Warm touches BEFORE the connection request: view profile (day 1), like a recent post (day 1-2), request (day 2-3), first message 4-24h after acceptance, follow-up day 5-7. [alex-vacca/linkedin-campaign-complete]
- LinkedIn activity hygiene: 9 AM-6 PM in the PROSPECT's timezone, weekdays only (Mon-Thu best), at least one full day off per week, no sudden volume spikes, varied copy. [alex-vacca/linkedin-campaign-complete]
- Spend the request budget by conversion likelihood: Tier 1 = event/webinar attendees, ICP-filtered post engagers, page followers, profile viewers; Tier 2 = new group members, newsletter subscribers, commenters; filter "posted within last 30 days" so requests do not go to dead accounts. [alex-vacca/linkedin-campaign-complete]
- Phone no-show recovery assumes good intent and offers one alternative slot; voicemail stays under 20 seconds and ends "I'll shoot you an email with some times", handing off to the email thread instead of asking for a callback. [alex-vacca/cold-call-scripts]
