## Unvalidated — external, not yet run by Jan
> Filed by the ColdIQ radar from third-party skills. Attributed; NOT gtm-master doctrine.
> A bullet moves into the body above only after it has been run in a real campaign and worked.

- Before any copy, generate exactly three competing angles per persona as testable units, each with: core tension (why now, 1 sentence), why it works, pain in their words, 10-20 word hook, 3-5 email arc, "best used when" signal conditions, "avoid" traps. Copy is written per angle, not per persona. [l3mpire/campaign-angle-finder]
- The read/delete decision is made on subject -> sender name -> first line only, so the sender-name string is a copy decision to set and test deliberately, not a mailbox default. [l3mpire/copywriting-first-touch]
- For IC recipients the objective is internal championing, not a booked meeting: the late touch reframes the ask around the IC being the person who spotted and solved the problem for their manager (career recognition), escalating CTA curiosity -> direct -> recognition. [l3mpire/copywriting-ic-sequence]
