# Creator-Radar Objections: 2026 Blocker & Reframe Tactics

## When to load
Late-stage deal blockers, CFO/security vetoes, pricing before the call, cold-call source objections, build-vs-buy pushback. Triggers: "what still needs to happen", "CFO veto", "send pricing before the call", "where did you get my number", "we can build this ourselves", "build it on Claude".

## Surface blockers early
- **Late-stage blocker question:** (single-case: M&A) before exclusivity or signature ask "What still needs to happen before you're comfortable wiring the money?" to list remaining milestones early. [Gazdecki #657]
- **Pre-empt finance and security on call one:** half of buyers had an approved deal vetoed by the CFO, so the CFO business case and security review go in the first sales call. [Vacca #1377]
- **Counter-view (2026-10-11):** disputes `cold-email/knowledge/reframe-offer.md` "Never put the price in the email". After a meeting is booked, send the offer doc WITH pricing before the call: non-buyers self-filter, no sticker shock, the call closes. Cold email stays price-free. [Lakajev #938, #1183]

## Reframes
- **"Where did you get my number?":** split it into source (answer honestly, one line) and reason for calling (the real job). If the reason is weak, the objection is a prep failure. [Douablin #1129]
- **"We can build this on Claude":** agree it is buildable, then sell the thousands of product decisions already made; nobody buys development hours. Swan tripled win rate on these deals. [Waxman #1712]
