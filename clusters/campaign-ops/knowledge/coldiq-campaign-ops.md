### Campaign diagnosis
- Fixed triage ladder for an underperforming campaign, fixed upstream before downstream: (1) deliverability/warmup -> (2) reply rate -> (3) LinkedIn accept rate -> (4) positive reply rate -> (5) open rate (weak signal, last). Do not touch copy while a lower rung is broken. [l3mpire/outbound-analyst]

### Idea stress-test (before a motion is built)
- State the bet as "if we [action], then [outcome], because [mechanism]", then grade every stated and unstated assumption Validated / Reasonable / Unproven / Risky before expanding it; build-hypothesis-matrix produces hypotheses but never audits what sits under them. [l3mpire/gtm-action-thinker]
- Run the idea against a fixed blind-spot list (confirmation bias, execution complexity, ICP drift toward the persona you like rather than the one that buys, channel saturation, attribution trap, sequencing error, resource mismatch, timing miss), plus one mandatory strongest-case-against pass. [l3mpire/gtm-action-thinker]
- Declare kill criteria before launch: "if [metric] < [threshold] after [timeframe], stop and pivot", with leading indicators (visible week 1-2) split from lagging (week 3-6). Prevents running a motion past its signal on sunk cost. [l3mpire/gtm-action-thinker]
- Expand an accepted idea in three directions: deepen (10x version of the same idea), adjacent (same idea x other segment / channel / inbound-outbound inversion / automation / partner), contrarian inversion (opposite persona, channel or timing). [l3mpire/gtm-action-thinker]
- Execution plan = MVP first; dependencies triaged Already resolved / Easy / Hard blocker; week-by-week map with one deliverable per week; name what breaks at 10x and must be solved now. [l3mpire/gtm-action-thinker]

### Meeting prep (booked meeting -> AE)
- Pre-call brief per meeting, hard 500-word cap: exec summary, company snapshot, prospect profile, 3 conversation starters, 5 discovery questions, 3 objection/handle pairs, time-blocked agenda, success metrics. Fills the gap between score-meeting-intent and the AE handoff. [janskuba/meeting-prep]
- Question/positioning ratio set by meeting type: discovery = 80/20 (30 min: 0-3 rapport, 3-15 discovery, 15-22 positioning, 22-27 next steps, 27-30 recap); demo = 30/70 (60 min: 0-5, 5-15 discovery, 15-45 demo, 45-55, 55-60). [janskuba/meeting-prep]
- No BANT in discovery (budget, decision maker, timeline, competing vendors); ask instead for the impact of the pain, their picture of "good", and the cost of inaction. [janskuba/meeting-prep]
- Write 2-3 named success outcomes before the call (e.g. "contact volunteered an internal champion") so the meeting is scored pass/fail, not by feel. [janskuba/meeting-prep]
