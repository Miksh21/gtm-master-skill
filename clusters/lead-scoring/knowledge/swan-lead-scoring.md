- Expansion depth counts only what the customer deliberately built (integrations beyond defaults, automations beyond the default one, usage volume, multiple active users); anything onboarding auto-creates exists on every account and scores nothing. [ariel-cohen/customer-expansion-scoring]
- Inactivity cap: zero usage signals in 30 days caps the tier at second-lowest whatever else is true, with churn risk flagged; churn risk outranks expansion scoring. [ariel-cohen/customer-expansion-scoring]
- Senior-stakeholder exception: a Director+/VP/C-level registered user at a quality company ($10M+ funded, 100+ employees or a known brand) scores second-highest tier regardless of seats, because that person can unlock budget and teammates. [ariel-cohen/customer-expansion-scoring]
- Expansion signal ranking: completed meeting (new departments, budget talk, advanced use cases) > product depth > advanced support/chat questions > post-signup site visits (pricing = upgrade signal) > engagement with founder content. [ariel-cohen/customer-expansion-scoring]
