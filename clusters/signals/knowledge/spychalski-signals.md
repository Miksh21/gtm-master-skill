- Champion tracking: seed the watched list only with past contacts of neutral-or-positive sentiment; the play converts best for tools people re-buy out of habit. Author reports 90%+ conversion across clients (unverified). [spychalski/gtm-engineering-2026]
- Mine job listings as text, not as a boolean: aggregate the wording across a company's listings (new tech to ramp, new initiative, new region) to infer internal priorities; historical listings add trend (Centreion is one source, or build it). [spychalski/gtm-engineering-2026]
- PLG first-party signal: detect company-clustered free signups (e.g. 15 users from one company) or feature-pair usage that predicts a further purchase, and route to an enterprise consolidation/upsell motion. [spychalski/gtm-engineering-2026]
- Keep firmographic fit score and signal score separate: fit buckets accounts, only the signal score may fire a rep notification; rank signals by funnel depth (site visit > fresh raise; "hiring for a CRM" > "raised, will need a CRM") to stop alert fatigue. [spychalski/gtm-engineering-2026]
