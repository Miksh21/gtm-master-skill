# Go-To-Market Alpha — The Signal Originality Test

Most "buying signals" are commodities: funding, hiring, job changes, tech installs. Anyone can buy them off the shelf, so everyone reaches out on the same trigger at the same time → signal fatigue. **Go-to-market alpha** is a signal derived from *your* product's specific value or *your* first-party data that no competitor can replicate — the thing that makes a cold message land because only you could have noticed it.

## When to load

- Designing a signal play and you want an *edge*, not table stakes
- "Our signal-led campaign isn't outperforming cold" (the signal probably isn't alpha)
- Building a proprietary signal from first-party / product / CRM data

## The test

> **Can a competitor buy this exact signal off the shelf?**
> - **Yes** → table stakes. Use it, but it won't differentiate you.
> - **No** → alpha. This is where to invest.

## The spectrum

| | Table-stakes signal | Go-to-market alpha |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Bought (Apollo, Bombora, news) | Derived from your product value or first-party data |
| Who has it | Everyone in your category | Only you |
| Reply lift | Modest, decays with fatigue | High and durable |
| Examples | Funding, hiring, job change, tech install | (below) |

## Worked examples

- **Canva** — compares a prospect's *public brand guidelines* to their *latest social post*; a mismatch is the signal that the brand isn't being applied consistently → reach out with the exact product that fixes it. No competitor computes this.
- **Mercado (cameras/compliance)** — agents detect a *compliance-rule violation* that legally requires the sensor they sell. The signal **is** the value prop.
- **An IT-recruitment client (anonymized) — two proven first-party signals (from the live audit):**
  - *Ex-employer placement signal* — HR at a company where you recently placed a candidate. Pure first-party relationship data; competitors can't see it. Measured **16.9% reply rate** vs 3–5% on generic HR lists.
  - *Active job-posting signal* — HR at a company posting a role you can fill, scraped from the market's biggest board. **15.9% reply rate.**

## How to derive your own

1. Name the single pain your product *uniquely* removes.
2. Ask: what *observable* event proves a company has that pain right now?
3. Find the data only you (or few) can see it in — product telemetry, your CRM/placement history, a bespoke scrape, a computed comparison.
4. Make the signal the **spine of the message** (lead with what you noticed), not a decoration on line one.

## Anti-patterns

- Buying the same intent feed as everyone and calling it "signal-led."
- A signal the copy never actually references — the same client's US A/B ran a "hiring" trigger at **0.17%** vs **0.22%** no-trigger (no lift), because the signal wasn't leveraged in copy *and* the market didn't fit.
- One alpha signal applied to the wrong business model / buyer (see `philosophy.md` → business-model fit).
