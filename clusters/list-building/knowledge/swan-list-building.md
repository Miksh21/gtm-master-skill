> A bullet moves into the body above only after it has been run in a real campaign and worked.

- Expect 30-60% row overlap when combining Apollo + Sales Nav + Clay; merge field by field, keeping the most recently VERIFIED value and breaking ties by that source's historical accuracy for that field. [alex-vacca/list-architect]
- Two extra pre-launch gates: at least 70% email coverage before launch, and competitor customers plus the do-not-contact list checked as explicit checklist items. [alex-vacca/list-building-tips]
- Map a named account's committee from relationship history BEFORE any prospect search: CRM contacts and deals, each sender's SENT folder filtered to the account domain, meeting transcripts by participant domain, prior sequences (reached, replied, booked), merged to one row per person. [ido-goldberg/map-contacts]
- Relationship strength per person: cold / warm-via-X / former contact who moved / former contact still there / current with history / net-new with no history; net-new rows are labelled "no relationship history" so they read differently from warm paths. [ido-goldberg/map-contacts]
- Movers first: every past contact who left gets a current-employer check, and a closed-lost champion now at a target account should top the list; new-to-role current contacts are flagged as warm paths; the stated intent (net-new ABM, renewal prep, champion tracking, de-single-threading) shifts the weighting. [ido-goldberg/map-contacts]
- Gap honesty: personas with no or weak coverage are flagged at the TOP; enrich only the slice about to be acted on, never every net-new contact; map strictly to the saved persona model, never invent personas. [ido-goldberg/map-contacts]
- (single-case: one vendor's partner-meeting workflow) Inward portfolio pass: match a fund's portfolio companies by email domain against your own CRM/warehouse (paying status, plan mix, usage, open pipeline) to prep a partner meeting; the same pass yields the greenfield list (portcos not using you) for outward prospecting. [brad-smith/vc-portfolio-research]
- (single-case: one vendor's partner-meeting workflow) Demo-safe split: the partner-facing doc carries zero financial figures (penetration, plan tiers, usage, open opportunities without amounts); ARR and account IDs live in a separate internal sheet; nothing is shared externally without explicit confirmation. [brad-smith/vc-portfolio-research]
- (single-case: one vendor's partner-meeting workflow) Keep a portfolio registry (firm, date, doc links, portco, domain, account ID) upserted every run so re-runs and pipeline-overlap analysis start from it instead of re-scraping. [brad-smith/vc-portfolio-research]
- Ambiguous domain matches are disclosed in their own appendix, never silently counted. [brad-smith/vc-portfolio-research]
